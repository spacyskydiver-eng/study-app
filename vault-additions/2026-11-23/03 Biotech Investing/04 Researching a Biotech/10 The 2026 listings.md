## Points that stand out
- **Runway versus readout**: Hemab's and SpyGlass's Phase 3 trials finish in 2029, beyond the adjusted runway once Phase 3 spending rises, so more fundraising is likely before results
- **Apnimed** is the only one near approval, and it already has revenue from a joint venture with Shionogi; its 9-month result was a profit only because of a one-off accounting gain
- **ADARx and Parabilis** hold the most cash relative to spending, so less near-term dilution risk
- **Kailera** burns the most ($93m a quarter) in the most competitive market (obesity)

## Questions
> [!question]- Which of the ten is closest to approval?
> Apnimed (APMD): AD109 has finished Phase 3 and is under FDA review.

> [!question]- Why is Hemab likely to raise money before its Phase 3 result?
> Its SOLARA Phase 3 finishes around Nov 2029, but once Phase 3 spending rises its cash lasts only about 2 years.
