# The research method

> [!path] [[Biotech Investing]] › [[Researching a Biotech]]

## The eight steps
| Step | Question | Where the answer is | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What does the company do, in one sentence? | Prospectus, "Business" / "Overview" | 10 min |
| 2 | Is the science believable? | Prospectus "Our programs", papers on PubMed | 30 to 60 min |
| 3 | Who else is trying to solve this, and what does the drug have to beat? | Prospectus "Competition", [[Standard of care]] | 20 min |
| 4 | How much cash, how fast is it spent, how long does it last? | Latest [[Form 10-Q]], balance sheet and cash-flow statement | 15 min |
| 5 | What happens next, and when? | ClinicalTrials.gov, 10-Q "Recent developments" | 15 min |
| 6 | What is the market already paying for it? | Share price × shares = [[Market capitalisation]], minus cash = [[Enterprise value]] | 10 min |
| 7 | What could go wrong? | Prospectus and 10-Q "Risk factors" | 20 min |
| 8 | Write the [[Investment thesis]] and the sell rules, then decide | A note of your own | 15 min |

- About 2 to 3 hours for a first pass on one company
- Most companies fail at step 2, 4 or 6: weak science, short cash, or already expensive. Stop there and move on; most research ends in "no"

## Long-term versus trading
- A long-term holding is a bet that the **company** will be worth much more in 3 to 10 years: a drug approved and selling, or a takeover
- Short-term price moves around news are mostly noise for this; [[07 Do events give you an edge?|events rarely give an edge]]
- The questions that matter long-term: will the drug work, will doctors use it, can the company survive until it sells, and is the price today reasonable for that

## Questions
> [!question]- Which three steps rule out most companies?
> Step 2 (the science is weak), step 4 (the cash runs out soon) and step 6 (the price already assumes success).

> [!question]- What is a long-term biotech investment a bet on?
> That the company will be worth much more in 3 to 10 years, usually because a drug is approved and sells, or the company is bought.
