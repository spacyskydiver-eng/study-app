# Recap, protonation and pH

> [!path] [[Biological Chemistry]] › [[Buffers]]

*Source: Biological Chemistry, Chemistry in Aqueous Environment: Importance of Buffers, Dr Maria Vardakou (PowerPoint); Crowe and Bradshaw, Chemistry for the Biosciences, slides 2-6*

- This lecture builds on the end of [[Acids and Bases|Lecture 8]]
- Adding acid protonates a weak acid (HA) or weak base (BH⁺); adding base deprotonates it (A⁻, B)
- Below its pKa a group is **protonated**; above its pKa it is **deprotonated**
  - acids: protonated = neutral, deprotonated = charged
  - bases: protonated = charged, deprotonated = neutral

## Questions
> [!question]- A weak base is in solution at a pH below its pKa. Is it charged?
> Yes: it is protonated (BH⁺), so positively charged.
