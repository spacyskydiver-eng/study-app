# Why we need buffers

> [!path] [[Biological Chemistry]] › [[Buffers]]

*Source: Biological Chemistry, Chemistry in Aqueous Environment: Importance of Buffers, Dr Maria Vardakou (PowerPoint); Crowe and Bradshaw, Chemistry for the Biosciences, slides 18-19*

## In the body
- Natural buffers maintain **homeostasis**. Blood pH outside its normal range causes **acidosis** (headache, confusion, breathlessness, arrhythmia, seizures) or **alkalosis** (confusion, tremor, tingling, muscle spasms)
- The **bicarbonate buffer** in blood:

> CO₂(g) + H₂O ⇌ H₂CO₃ ⇌ H₃O⁺ + HCO₃⁻

- The **imidazole** side chain of histidine buffers near neutral pH (pKa about 6), which is why histidine is common in proteins and enzyme active sites

## In the lab
- Keep **proteins and cells stable**
- Run an experiment at its **optimum pH**
- Stop added reagents or products from **changing the conditions** of the experiment
