# Amphoteric species

> [!path] [[Biological Chemistry]] › [[Acids and Bases]]

*Source: Biological Chemistry, Chemistry in Aqueous Environment: Acids and Bases, Dr Maria Vardakou (PowerPoint); Crowe and Bradshaw, Chemistry for the Biosciences, ch. 17, slides 11-12*

- **[[Amphoteric]]** species can act as **either an acid or a base**
- **Water**:
  - HCl + H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻: water **accepts** a proton, so it is a **base**
  - NH₃ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄⁺ + OH⁻: water **donates** a proton, so it is an **acid**
- **Amino acids**: the **carboxyl group** (–COOH) can donate H⁺ and the **amino group** (–NH₂) can accept H⁺, so they are amphoteric too

## Questions
> [!question]- What does amphoteric mean?
> Able to act as an acid or a base.

> [!question]- In NH₃ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄⁺ + OH⁻, is water acting as an acid or a base?
> An acid: it donates a proton to NH₃.

> [!question]- Which groups make an amino acid amphoteric?
> The carboxyl group (proton donor) and the amino group (proton acceptor).
