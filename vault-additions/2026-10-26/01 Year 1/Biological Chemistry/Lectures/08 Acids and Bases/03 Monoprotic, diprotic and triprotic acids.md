# Monoprotic, diprotic and triprotic acids

> [!path] [[Biological Chemistry]] › [[Acids and Bases]]

*Source: Biological Chemistry, Chemistry in Aqueous Environment: Acids and Bases, Dr Maria Vardakou (PowerPoint); Crowe and Bradshaw, Chemistry for the Biosciences, ch. 17, slides 8-9*

Classified by how many moles of protons one mole of acid can donate:

| Type | Protons per molecule | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monoprotic** | 1 | HCl, HNO₃, CH₃COOH |
| **Diprotic** | 2 | H₂SO₄, oxalic acid (HOOCCOOH) |
| **Triprotic** | 3 | H₃PO₄ |

- Polyprotic acids lose their protons **one at a time**:
  - H₂SO₄ + H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + HSO₄⁻, then HSO₄⁻ + H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + SO₄²⁻
  - overall: H₂SO₄ + 2H₂O ⇌ 2H₃O⁺ + SO₄²⁻
  - H₃PO₄ + 3H₂O ⇌ 3H₃O⁺ + PO₄³⁻

## Questions
> [!question]- Classify HCl, H₂SO₄ and H₃PO₄ by proton yield.
> HCl monoprotic, H₂SO₄ diprotic, H₃PO₄ triprotic.

> [!question]- Write the two dissociation steps of sulfuric acid.
> H₂SO₄ + H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + HSO₄⁻; HSO₄⁻ + H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + SO₄²⁻.
