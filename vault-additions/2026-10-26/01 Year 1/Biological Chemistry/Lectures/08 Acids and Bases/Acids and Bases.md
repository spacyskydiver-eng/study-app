> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | Brønsted-Lowry | acid = proton donor · base = proton acceptor |
> | pH | −log₁₀[H₃O⁺]; 1 pH unit = 10× [H₃O⁺] |
> | Kw | [H₃O⁺][OH⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴ at 25 °C; pH + pOH = 14 |

> | Strength | lower pKa = stronger acid |
> | Strong acid | pH = −log[acid] |
> | Weak acid | ICE table: Ka = x² / ([HA] − x), x = [H₃O⁺] |
> | pH < pKa | protonated (acids neutral, bases charged) |
> | pH > pKa | deprotonated (acids charged, bases neutral) |
