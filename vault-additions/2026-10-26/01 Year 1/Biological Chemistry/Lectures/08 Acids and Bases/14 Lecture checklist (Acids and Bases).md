# Lecture checklist (Acids and Bases)

> [!path] [[Biological Chemistry]] › [[Acids and Bases]]

*Source: Biological Chemistry, Chemistry in Aqueous Environment: Acids and Bases, Dr Maria Vardakou (PowerPoint); Crowe and Bradshaw, Chemistry for the Biosciences, ch. 17, slides 52*

- [ ] Define acids, bases and pH (Arrhenius and Brønsted-Lowry)
- [ ] Use the pH scale, and convert between pH, pOH, [H₃O⁺] and [OH⁻]
- [ ] Tell strong from weak acids and bases, and write their dissociation equations in water
- [ ] Identify conjugate acid-base pairs
- [ ] Explain Ka, Kb, pKa and pKb and how they relate
- [ ] Calculate the pH of strong and weak acids and bases
- [ ] Predict whether a group is protonated or charged from pH and pKa
