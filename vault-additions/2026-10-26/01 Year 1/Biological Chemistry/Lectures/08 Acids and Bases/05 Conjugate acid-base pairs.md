# Conjugate acid-base pairs

> [!path] [[Biological Chemistry]] › [[Acids and Bases]]

*Source: Biological Chemistry, Chemistry in Aqueous Environment: Acids and Bases, Dr Maria Vardakou (PowerPoint); Crowe and Bradshaw, Chemistry for the Biosciences, ch. 17, slides 13-15*

- When an acid loses H⁺ it becomes its **[[Conjugate base|conjugate base]]**; when a base gains H⁺ it becomes its **[[Conjugate acid|conjugate acid]]**
- **Every acid-base reaction contains two conjugate pairs**:

| Reaction | Acid / conjugate base | Base / conjugate acid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HA + H₂O ⇌ A⁻ + H₃O⁺ | HA / A⁻ | H₂O / H₃O⁺ |
| CH₃COOH + H₂O ⇌ CH₃COO⁻ + H₃O⁺ | CH₃COOH / CH₃COO⁻ | H₂O / H₃O⁺ |
| NH₃ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄⁺ + OH⁻ | H₂O / OH⁻ | NH₃ / NH₄⁺ |
| B + H₂O ⇌ BH⁺ + OH⁻ | H₂O / OH⁻ | B / BH⁺ |

- Read it in both directions: in the back reaction NH₄⁺ is the acid and OH⁻ the base

## Questions
> [!question]- What is the conjugate base of CH₃COOH?
> CH₃COO⁻ (acetate).

> [!question]- Identify both conjugate pairs in NH₃ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄⁺ + OH⁻.
> NH₄⁺/NH₃ and H₂O/OH⁻.

> [!question]- What is the conjugate acid of water?
> H₃O⁺.
