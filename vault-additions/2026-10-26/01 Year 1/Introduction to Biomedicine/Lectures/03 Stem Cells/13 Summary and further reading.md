# Summary and further reading

> [!path] [[Introduction to Biomedicine]] › [[Stem Cells]]

*Source: Introduction to Biomedicine, Grand Challenges: Stem cells, Dr Mark Williams (PowerPoint), slides 23-26*

- Stem cells **specialise** and **self-renew**
- Three main types are researched: **embryonic**, **tissue** and **induced pluripotent** stem cells, each with advantages and disadvantages
- Uses: **cell replacement therapy**, **drug discovery** and **drug toxicity testing**
- **Immune rejection** must be considered for every cell replacement therapy
- Different injuries and diseases may need different strategies; expectations are high, but challenges remain

## Further reading
- [NIH stem cell information](https://stemcells.nih.gov/)
- [EuroStemCell](http://www.eurostemcell.org/)
- Stem cells come back in Human Physiology, Cell Biology, Cancer Biology, Cell Biology and Disease, and Translational Biomedicine
