# Future prospects, iPS cell therapy

> [!path] [[Introduction to Biomedicine]] › [[Stem Cells]]

*Source: Introduction to Biomedicine, Grand Challenges: Stem cells, Dr Mark Williams (PowerPoint), slides 18*

Proof of principle in a mouse with sickle cell anaemia, and the same approach for **Fanconi anaemia**:

1. **Collect skin cells** from the affected mouse
2. **Reprogram** them into iPS cells with viruses carrying Oct4, Sox2, Klf4 and c-Myc
3. **Correct the mutation** in the iPS cells, giving genetically corrected iPS cells
4. **Differentiate** them into **blood stem cells**
5. **Transplant** them back into the mouse, which recovers

- The cells are the patient's own (so no rejection) and the disease-causing mutation has been fixed
