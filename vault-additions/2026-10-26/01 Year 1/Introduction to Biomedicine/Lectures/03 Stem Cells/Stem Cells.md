# Stem Cells

> [!path] [[Introduction to Biomedicine]] › Lecture 3

> [!abstract] Lecture 3 · Grand Challenges
> 13 topics · 18 key terms · 31 questions

> [!key] At a glance
>
> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | Stem cell | self-renews and can specialise (differentiate) into other cell types |
> | Totipotent | zygote: every cell type, including the placenta |
> | Pluripotent | inner cell mass / ES cells: every body cell type, not the placenta |
> | Multipotent | tissue stem cells: only the cell types of their own tissue |
> | iPS cells | adult cells reprogrammed with Oct4, Sox2, Klf4, c-Myc |
> | Germ layers | ectoderm, mesoderm, endoderm form at gastrulation (week 3) |
> | Immune rejection | avoided if the cells come from the patient (tissue or iPS) |
> | UK law | HFE Act 1990 + HFEA licence; no embryo kept past 14 days |
