# Stem cells and immune rejection

> [!path] [[Introduction to Biomedicine]] › [[Stem Cells]]

*Source: Introduction to Biomedicine, Grand Challenges: Stem cells, Dr Mark Williams (PowerPoint), slides 15*

- **[[Immune rejection]]** matters for every cell replacement therapy
- **ES cells**: would be recognised as foreign, because donor and recipient are genetically different
- **Tissue stem cells** and **iPS cells**:
  - taken from the **patient's own body**, no rejection
  - taken from a **donor**, immune-suppressing drugs are needed

> [!figure] Immune rejection risk for ES cells, tissue stem cells and iPS cells (Lecture 3, slide 15)
> ![[ib3_s15.jpg]]

## Questions
> [!question]- Why would ES cell therapies risk rejection?
> The cells are genetically different from the recipient, so the immune system sees them as foreign.

> [!question]- When do tissue stem cell or iPS cell therapies need immune-suppressing drugs?
> When the starting cells come from a donor rather than the patient.
