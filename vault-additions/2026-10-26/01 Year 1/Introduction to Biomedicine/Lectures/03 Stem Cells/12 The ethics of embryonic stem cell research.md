# The ethics of embryonic stem cell research

> [!path] [[Introduction to Biomedicine]] › [[Stem Cells]]

*Source: Introduction to Biomedicine, Grand Challenges: Stem cells, Dr Mark Williams (PowerPoint), slides 32-33*

- Using early human embryos is a complex and controversial ethical issue
- Three views of an embryo's **moral status**:
  1. **No moral status**
  2. **The same moral status as a baby**
  3. **An intermediate view** between the two
- The **[[14-day rule]]** draws a line, but opinion is divided on whether such a line can be drawn
- In the UK, embryos can be **created for research** in specific circumstances: **animal-human hybrid embryos** and **nuclear replacement**
- Further issues: using ES cells for **drug and toxicity testing**, and making **sex cells** (sperm and eggs)
