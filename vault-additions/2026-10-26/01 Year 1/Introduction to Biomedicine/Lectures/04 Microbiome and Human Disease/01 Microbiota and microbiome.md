# Microbiota and microbiome

> [!path] [[Introduction to Biomedicine]] › [[Microbiome and Human Disease]]

*Source: Introduction to Biomedicine, Grand Challenges: Microbiome and Human Disease, Dr Sean Tattan (PowerPoint), slides 1-3*

- **[[Microbiota]]**: the microorganisms that live in an established environment
- **[[Microbiome]]**: the full complement of microbes, **their genes and genomes**, in a particular environment
- By the end of the lecture you should be able to:
  - describe the composition and normal functions of the microbiome
  - explain how microbes influence host physiology
  - compare the evidence for microbiome involvement across several diseases

## Questions
> [!question]- What is the difference between the microbiota and the microbiome?
> The microbiota is the microorganisms themselves; the microbiome is the microbes plus all their genes and genomes.
