# Where our microbes live

> [!path] [[Introduction to Biomedicine]] › [[Microbiome and Human Disease]]

*Source: Introduction to Biomedicine, Grand Challenges: Microbiome and Human Disease, Dr Sean Tattan (PowerPoint), slides 4-6*

- Sites with a **[[Normal flora|normal flora]]**: skin and mucous membranes, upper respiratory tract, gastrointestinal tract, outer opening of the urethra, external genitalia, vagina, external ear canal, and the external eye (lids, conjunctiva)
- In the gut, a **mucus layer** keeps most bacteria in the lumen, away from the mucosa
- The human microbiome has **over 1,000,000 genes**; the human genome has about **23,000**

## Every domain is represented

| Domain | Example | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Bacteria]] | *Staphylococcus epidermidis* | skin |
| Eukarya ([[Fungi|fungi]]) | *Candida albicans* | gut, vagina, mouth |
| Archaea | *Methanobrevibacter smithii* | gut |
| [[Virus|Viruses]] | (bacteriophages and others) | throughout |

## Questions
> [!question]- Name four body sites that carry a normal flora.
> Any four of: skin, mucous membranes, upper respiratory tract, GI tract, urethral opening, external genitalia, vagina, ear canal, external eye.

> [!question]- Roughly how many genes are in the human microbiome compared with the human genome?
> Over 1,000,000 against about 23,000.

> [!question]- Give an archaeon and a eukaryote from the human microbiome.
> Methanobrevibacter smithii (gut archaeon) and Candida albicans (a fungus).
