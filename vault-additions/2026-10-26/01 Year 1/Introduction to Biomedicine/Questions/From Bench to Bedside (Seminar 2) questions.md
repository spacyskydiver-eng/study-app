# From Bench to Bedside (Seminar 2): questions

> [!path] [[Introduction to Biomedicine]] › [[From Bench to Bedside (Seminar 2)]]

## 6 questions

### Seminar 1 recap

> [!question]- How many compounds typically enter drug discovery for one approved drug?
> About 5,000 to 10,000.

> [!question]- What two events activate CFTR?
> Phosphorylation of the R domain and ATP binding at NBD1 and NBD2.

### What makes an effective scientific presentation

> [!question]- What structure does Dr Tattan suggest for a scientific talk?
> Background → why the topic matters / the gap → methods and results from 2 to 4 key papers → conclusion.

> [!question]- Give two design rules for presentation slides.
> High contrast and consistent layout with a colour-blind-friendly scheme; large fonts; minimal text; clear, enlarged figures.

### Primary literature to guide your preparation

> [!question]- What did Schwank et al. (2013) show?
> CRISPR/Cas9 corrected the F508del CFTR mutation in patient intestinal organoids, restoring forskolin-induced swelling.

> [!question]- What is the difference between integrating and non-integrating gene therapy vectors?
> Integrating vectors insert the gene into the genome so it is inherited by daughter cells; non-integrating ones do not, so expression is lost over time and re-dosing is needed.
