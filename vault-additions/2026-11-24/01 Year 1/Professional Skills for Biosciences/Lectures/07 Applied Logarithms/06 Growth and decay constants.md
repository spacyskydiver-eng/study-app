# Growth and decay constants

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Applied Logarithms (Maths 5)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Applied Logarithms, Dr Beth Williams (PowerPoint), slides 26-34*

> [!key] Exponential growth
> **N = N₀eᵏᵗ** (N number, N₀ number at t = 0, k growth or decay constant, t time)
> → N/N₀ = eᵏᵗ → **ln(N/N₀) = kt** → **k = ln(N/N₀) / t**

| Example | Working | k |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 mice → 125 in 160 days | ln(125/30) / 160 | **0.0089 day⁻¹** |
| Measles cases double in 1 year | ln 2 / 1 | **0.693 year⁻¹** |
| Parasites 250 → 3600 mL⁻¹ in 3 days | ln(14.4) / 3 | **0.889 day⁻¹** |

## Decay
- Same equation for isotope decay; k is the **decay constant** and is **negative**
- Growth: N > N₀ → ln(N/N₀) > 0 → **k > 0**; decay: N < N₀ → ln(N/N₀) < 0 → **k < 0**
- Half-life 6 h: k = ln(0.5) / 6 = **−0.116 h⁻¹**
- k = −0.2 day⁻¹: t½ = ln(0.5) / −0.2 = **3.47 days**
- Find N after time t: N = N₀ × e^(kt), e.g. 200 × e^(0.05 × 10) = 200 × 1.649 = 330

## Questions
> [!question]- 30 mice become 125 in 160 days. Growth constant?
> ln(125/30)/160 = 0.0089 day⁻¹.

> [!question]- An isotope has a half-life of 6 h. Decay constant?
> ln 0.5 / 6 = −0.116 h⁻¹.

> [!question]- k = −0.2 day⁻¹. Half-life?
> ln 0.5 / −0.2 = 3.47 days.
