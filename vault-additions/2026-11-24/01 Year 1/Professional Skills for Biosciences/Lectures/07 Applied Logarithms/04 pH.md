# pH

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Applied Logarithms (Maths 5)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Applied Logarithms, Dr Beth Williams (PowerPoint), slides 17-18*

- pH ("potential of hydrogen") measures **[H⁺]** (or [H₃O⁺]), which ranges about 10⁻¹⁴ to 1 mol L⁻¹
- **pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]**; the minus sign makes pH positive when [H⁺] < 1 M (pH can be negative if [H⁺] > 1 M)
- **1 pH unit = 10-fold change** in [H⁺]

| [H⁺] (M) | pH |
| --- | --- |
| 0.1 | 1 |
| 10⁻⁷ | 7 |
| 4 × 10⁻¹⁰ | 9.40 |
- Back: [H⁺] = 10⁻ᵖᴴ

## Questions
> [!question]- pH when [H⁺] = 4 × 10⁻¹⁰ M?
> −log(4 × 10⁻¹⁰) = 9.40.
