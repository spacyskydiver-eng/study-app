# Molar mass and moles

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Moles and Molarity (Maths 4)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Moles and Molarity (PowerPoint), slides 15-21*

> [!key] Moles
> **moles (mol) = mass (g) / molar mass (g mol⁻¹)**
> molar mass = mass / moles; mass = molar mass × moles

| Workbook question | Working | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 mol KOH weigh 112.22 g; Mr? | 112.22 / 2 | 56.11 g mol⁻¹ |
| 0.5 mol NaCl weigh 29.22 g; Mr? | 29.22 / 0.5 | 58.44 g mol⁻¹ |
| Moles in 3.2 mg NaCl (58.44) | 3.2 × 10⁻³ / 58.44 | 5.48 × 10⁻⁵ mol |
| Moles in 1.4 µg glucose (180) | 1.4 × 10⁻⁶ / 180 | 7.78 × 10⁻⁹ mol |
| Mass of 0.25 µmol ZnCl₂ (136) | 0.25 × 10⁻⁶ × 136 | 3.4 × 10⁻⁵ g (34 µg) |
- Always convert mg/µg to **g** first

## Questions
> [!question]- How many moles are in 3.2 mg of NaCl (Mr 58.44)?
> 3.2 × 10⁻³ / 58.44 = 5.48 × 10⁻⁵ mol.
