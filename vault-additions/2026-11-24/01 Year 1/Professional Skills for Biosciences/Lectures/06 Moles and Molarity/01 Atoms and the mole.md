# Atoms and the mole

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Moles and Molarity (Maths 4)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Moles and Molarity (PowerPoint), slides 2-14*

- Elements are defined by their **proton number** (atomic number): ¹²₆C has 6 protons; mass number 12 = protons + neutrons (nucleons)
- A ¹²C atom has a mass of about **1.99 × 10⁻²³ g**: far too small to weigh out
- A **mole** of anything contains **6.022 × 10²³** particles (**Avogadro's number**, L or Nₐ); abbreviation **mol**
- 6.022 × 10²³ × 1.9943 × 10⁻²³ g = **12.01 g**, so one mole of carbon atoms weighs about 12 g
- The mass of a mole of an element ≈ its **mass number in grams**
