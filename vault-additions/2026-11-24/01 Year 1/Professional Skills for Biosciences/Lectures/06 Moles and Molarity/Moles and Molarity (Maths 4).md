> [!key] At a glance

> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | Mole | 6.022 × 10²³ particles (Avogadro’s number, L or Nₐ) |
> | Molar mass | mass of one mole, g mol⁻¹ (≈ mass number in grams for an element) |
> | Moles | n = mass (g) / molar mass (g mol⁻¹) |
> | Molarity | mol L⁻¹ = moles / volume (L); 1 mol L⁻¹ = 1 M |
> | Molarity from mass | M = mass / (molar mass × volume in L) |
> | Dilution | M₁V₁ = M₂V₂ |
> | % (w/v) | mass (g) / volume (mL) × 100 |
