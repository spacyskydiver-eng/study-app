# Dilutions with M₁V₁ = M₂V₂

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Moles and Molarity (Maths 4)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Moles and Molarity (PowerPoint), slides 27-30*

> [!key] Dilution
> **M₁V₁ = M₂V₂** (M₁, V₁ = stock; M₂, V₂ = solution being made); keep units the same on both sides

- 200 mL of 5 mM copper sulphate from 1 M stock: V₁ = M₂V₂ / M₁ = (5 × 10⁻³ × 0.2) / 1 = **0.001 L = 1 mL**, made up to 200 mL
- 0.7 L of 0.04 M NaCl from 0.25 M stock: V₁ = 0.04 × 0.7 / 0.25 = **0.112 L (112 mL)**
- 30 mL with 5 mM CaCl₂ and 80 mM MgSO₄ from 3.0 M and 1.5 M stocks: **0.05 mL CaCl₂ + 1.6 mL MgSO₄ + 28.35 mL water**
- 5 mM CaCl₂ in 500 mL from 1 M stock: **2.5 mL**

> [!note] Three formulae
> n = m / Mr · molarity = n / V · M₁V₁ = M₂V₂ (reading: *Maths for the Biosciences*, ch. 14)
