# Molarity

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Moles and Molarity (Maths 4)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Moles and Molarity (PowerPoint), slides 22-26*

> [!key] Molarity
> **molarity (mol L⁻¹) = moles / volume (L)** = mass / (molar mass × volume in L)

- Molarity applies to **solutions**; mol L⁻¹ is written **M** (mM = 10⁻³ M, µM = 10⁻⁶ M)
- 100 mL with 9.4 g Na₂CO₃ (106): 9.4 / (106 × 0.1) = **0.887 M**
- 0.25 mL with 0.58 mg NaCl (58.44): 0.58 × 10⁻³ / (58.44 × 0.25 × 10⁻³) = **0.0397 M (39.7 mM)**
- Mass of TRIS (121.1) for 200 mL of 1 M: 1 × 0.2 × 121.1 = **24.2 g**
- % (w/v) = mass (g) / volume (mL) × 100: 80 mL of 15 % glucose contains **12 g**
