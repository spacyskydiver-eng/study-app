# Order of operations (BODMAS)

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Rearranging Equations (Maths 1)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Rearranging Equations, Dr Beth Williams (PowerPoint) and Maths Basic Rules helpsheet, slides 3-6*

| Order | Operation |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **B**rackets ( ) |
| 2 | **O**rders: powers and roots (x², √) |
| 3 | **D**ivision and **M**ultiplication, equal rank, left to right |
| 4 | **A**ddition and **S**ubtraction, equal rank, left to right |

## Worked examples
- 4 + 5 × 32 − 2 = 4 + 160 − 2 = **162**
- 2 + 6 × (4 + 5) ÷ 3 − 5 = 2 + 6 × 9 ÷ 3 − 5 = 2 + 54 ÷ 3 − 5 = 2 + 18 − 5 = **15**

## Lecture practice (worked)
| Expression | Working | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 − 5 ÷ (8 − 3) × 2 + 5 | 4 − 5 ÷ 5 × 2 + 5 = 4 − 1 × 2 + 5 = 4 − 2 + 5 | **7** |
| 100 ÷ (6 + 7 × 2) − 5 | 100 ÷ 20 − 5 | **0** |
| 4 + (5 × 3² + 2) | 4 + (45 + 2) | **51** |
| 9 − 24 ÷ 8 × 2 + 3 | 9 − 3 × 2 + 3 = 9 − 6 + 3 | **6** |
| [(32 ÷ 4) + 3] × 2 | (8 + 3) × 2 | **22** |
