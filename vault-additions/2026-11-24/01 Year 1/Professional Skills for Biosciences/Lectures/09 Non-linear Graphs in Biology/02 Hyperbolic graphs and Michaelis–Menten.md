# Hyperbolic graphs and Michaelis–Menten

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Non-linear Graphs in Biology (Maths 7)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Non-linear Graphs in Biology (PowerPoint), slides 8-10*

- **y = ax/(b + x)**: the curve rises to a maximum reached only at infinity, approaching a horizontal line it never reaches (an **asymptote**)
- Enzyme kinetics: **V = Vmax[S] / (Km + [S])** (Michaelis–Menten)
  - **V** velocity of the reaction; **Vmax** maximum velocity; **[S]** substrate concentration
  - **Km** = [S] when V = Vmax/2 (similar to k in growth)
- Vmax is never actually reached, so Vmax and Km are found by **linearising**

> [!question]- Define Km.
> The substrate concentration at which V = Vmax/2.
