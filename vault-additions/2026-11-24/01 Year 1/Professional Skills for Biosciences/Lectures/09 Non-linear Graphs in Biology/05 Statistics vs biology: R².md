# Statistics vs biology: R²

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Non-linear Graphs in Biology (Maths 7)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Non-linear Graphs in Biology (PowerPoint), slide 22*

- **R²** shows how close the points are to the fitted line; the best possible is **1**
- Temperature vs % population increase: linear R² 0.857, cubic 0.912, quartic 0.929
- You can always draw a line (or a higher polynomial) of best fit, **but that doesn't make your data good**: choose the model that makes biological sense

## Questions
> [!question]- Does a higher R² from a more complex curve mean the model is right?
> No: more terms always fit better; the model must make biological sense.
