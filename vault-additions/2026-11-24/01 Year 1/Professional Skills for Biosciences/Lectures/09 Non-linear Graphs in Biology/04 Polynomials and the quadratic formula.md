# Polynomials and the quadratic formula

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Non-linear Graphs in Biology (Maths 7)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Non-linear Graphs in Biology (PowerPoint), slides 16-21*

- Quadratic: **y = ax² + bx + c**; key points: y-intercept (= c), x-intercepts (roots), the maximum or minimum, and x at that turning point
- a > 0: ∪ shape (minimum); a < 0: ∩ shape (maximum)

> [!key] Quadratic formula (y = 0)
> **x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a**: ± gives the two places the curve crosses the x-axis

- y = x² + 6x − 8: x = (−6 ± √(36 + 32))/2 = (−6 ± 8.25)/2 → **x = 1.12 or −7.12**
- Factorising: y = x² − 6x + 8 = (x − 4)(x − 2) → **x = 2 or 4**
- Turning point at **x = −b/2a**; substitute back for the max/min y (e.g. y = −0.064x² + 2.4977x + 2.3273 peaks at x = 19.5)
- Cubic y = ax³ + bx² + cx + d: two turning points where the line changes direction; d is a constant

## Questions
> [!question]- Roots of y = x² + 6x − 8?
> x = 1.12 or −7.12.

> [!question]- Roots of y = x² − 6x + 8?
> (x − 4)(x − 2): x = 2 or 4.
