# Where serial dilutions are used

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[CFU and Dilutions (Maths 3)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: CFU and Dilutions (PowerPoint, Colony Forming Units), slides 14-15*

- Quantification and assays: **CFUs**, spectrophotometry (keeping absorbance inside its limits)
- **Standard curves**: qPCR, protein quantification (e.g. BSA)
- **MIC** and toxicity: antibiotic susceptibility
- **Enzyme kinetics**
