# CFU and Dilutions (Maths 3)

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › Lecture 5

> [!abstract] Lecture 5
> 4 topics · 5 key terms · 5 questions

> [!key] At a glance
>
> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | CFU | colony-forming unit: each colony grew from one viable cell |
> | Viable count | CFU mL⁻¹ = (colonies × dilution factor) / volume plated (mL) |
> | Dilution factor | a positive number: a 10⁻⁵ dilution has dilution factor 10⁵ |
> | Volume plated | convert to mL: 100 µL = 0.1 mL; 10 µL = 0.01 mL |
> | Countable plate | about 30 to 300 colonies |
> | Limits | only culturable, live bacteria are counted (around 1 % of bacteria culture) |
> | Standard curve | plot only the linear range; read unknowns from y = mx + c |
