# Calibration curve example

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Linear Graphs in Biology (Maths 6)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Linear Graphs in Biology, Dr Beth Williams (PowerPoint), slide 26*

- Beta-carotene: 0.01 g → A = 0.8; 0.03 g → A = 2.0
- m = (2.0 − 0.8)/(0.03 − 0.01) = 1.2/0.02 = **60**
- c = 0.8 − 60 × 0.01 = **0.2**
- **y = 60x + 0.2** (absorbance = 60 × mass in g + 0.2)
