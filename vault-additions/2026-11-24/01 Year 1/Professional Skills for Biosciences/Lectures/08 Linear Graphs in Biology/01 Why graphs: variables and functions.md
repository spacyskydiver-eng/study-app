# Why graphs: variables and functions

> [!path] [[Professional Skills for Biosciences]] › [[Linear Graphs in Biology (Maths 6)]]

*Source: Mathematical Concepts: Linear Graphs in Biology, Dr Beth Williams (PowerPoint), slides 3-6*

- To measure a property: choose the property to **measure (y)**, **change** another in a controlled way **(x)**, keep all others **constant**
- Plotting finds the **function y = f(x)**, the relationship that lets you calculate and predict y from x
- e.g. plant height: 10, 20, 30, 40 days → 5, 10, 15, 20 mm
- "Does A depend on B?" Yes → A is **y (dependent)**, B is **x (independent)**

## Questions
> [!question]- In an experiment measuring enzyme rate at different temperatures, which is x?
> Temperature (the independent variable).
