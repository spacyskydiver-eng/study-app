# Summary (Chromosomes and Genomes)

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Chromosomes and Genomes (Lectures 12 to 14)]]

*Source: Lectures 12, 13, 14: Chromosomes and Genomes, Parts 1 to 3 (PowerPoint), slides 49*

- **Eukaryotic chromosomes** are highly compacted structures made of linear DNA molecules and proteins, including **histones**
- Eukaryotic chromosomes contain **linear arrays of genes**, but unique protein-coding genes are only a **small proportion** of each chromosome. In most eukaryotes **most of each chromosome is repetitive DNA**
- Eukaryotes differ in the number of copies of their chromosomes: **haploid** = 1 copy of each, **diploid** = 2 copies, and so on
- In humans, females have **two X** chromosomes and males an **X and a Y**. Sex is determined by the **presence or absence of SRY**
- **Dosage compensation**: in human females **one X is inactivated**
- **Gene mutations and chromosome aberrations** create genetic variation and drive **evolution**
