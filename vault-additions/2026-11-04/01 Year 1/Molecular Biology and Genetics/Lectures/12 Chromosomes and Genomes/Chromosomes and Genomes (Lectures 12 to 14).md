> [!key] At a glance

> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | Genetic material | DNA in all cellular life; viruses can be ssDNA, dsDNA, ssRNA (+/−) or dsRNA |
> | WGS | shotgun sequencing: random fragments, overlaps assembled into contigs |
> | E. coli | one circular chromosome, 4,639,221 bp, ~4300 genes; genes tightly packed, both strands |
> | Nucleosome | histone octamer (2 each of H2A, H2B, H3, H4) + 147 bp DNA |
> | Centromere position | metacentric, submetacentric, acrocentric, telocentric |
> | C value paradox | genome size does not track complexity; most eukaryotic DNA is repetitive |
> | Ploidy | euploidy = multiples of n; aneuploidy = 2n ± whole chromosomes |
> | SRY | on the Y; encodes testis-determining factor at 6–8 weeks |
> | X inactivation | XIST RNA coats one X in females (Barr body); random = Lyon hypothesis |
> | Point mutations | transition (purine↔purine), transversion (purine↔pyrimidine); missense, nonsense, frameshift |
