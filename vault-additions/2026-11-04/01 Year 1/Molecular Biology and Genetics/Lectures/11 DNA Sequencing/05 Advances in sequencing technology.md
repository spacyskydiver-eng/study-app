# Advances in sequencing technology

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[DNA Sequencing (Lecture 11)]]

*Source: Lecture 11: DNA Sequencing, Prof Richard Bowater (PowerPoint), slides 14-15*

- **Sequencing rate** (kb per day per machine) has risen from about **10 kb** with **manual gels** (1980) through **automated slab gels** and **capillary sequencers** to over **1,000,000,000 kb** with **next-generation sequencers** (2010)
- **Cost per kb** fell from about **$10** to under **$0.01**

| Era | Technology |
| --- | --- |
| 1980s | manual gels, radioactive labels |
| 1990s | automated slab gels |
| about 2000 to 2005 | capillary sequencers |
| after 2005 | next-generation sequencers |

> [!quote] Mardis (2013) Annu Rev Anal Chem
> Automated DNA sequencing instruments embody an elegant interplay among chemistry, engineering, software, and molecular biology.

> [!quote] Shendure et al. (2017) Nature
> Between 2007 and 2012, the raw, per-base cost of DNA sequencing plummeted by four orders of magnitude.
