# Summary (DNA Sequencing)

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[DNA Sequencing (Lecture 11)]]

*Source: Lecture 11: DNA Sequencing, Prof Richard Bowater (PowerPoint), slides 21*

- DNA sequencing technology is **rapidly evolving**
- Sequencing of large genomes became possible with:
  - production of **pure DNA polymerases**
  - increased **sensitivity of nucleic acid detection**
  - **automated robotic** technology
  - improved **computer power**
- Further advances are increasing speed and reducing size and cost, so it will soon be possible to sequence an individual human genome for **$1,000**
- Sequencing is used for:
  - **de novo genome sequencing**
  - **targeted re-sequencing**
  - **transcriptome profiling**

## Questions
> [!question]- Name four developments that made sequencing large genomes possible.
> Pure DNA polymerases, more sensitive nucleic acid detection, automated robotics and improved computer power.

> [!question]- Name three uses of DNA sequencing.
> De novo genome sequencing, targeted re-sequencing and transcriptome profiling.
