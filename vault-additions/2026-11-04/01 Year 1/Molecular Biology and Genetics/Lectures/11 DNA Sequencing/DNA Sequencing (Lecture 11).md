# DNA Sequencing (Lecture 11)

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › Lecture 11

> [!abstract] Lecture 11
> 10 topics · 10 key terms · 29 questions

> [!key] At a glance
>
> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | DNA fingerprinting | Alec Jeffreys, Leicester, 1980s; now PCR of STR loci + capillary electrophoresis |
> | qPCR | reverse transcriptase copies RNA to cDNA; fluorescence measures product in real time |
> | TaqMan | probe with reporter + quencher; Taq cleaves it, reporter fluoresces |
> | Sanger sequencing | 1977; DNA polymerase + dNTPs + a few ddNTPs that stop the chain |
> | ddNTP | no 3′-OH, so no next nucleotide can be added |
> | Human Genome Project | two first drafts 2001 (public HGP and Celera); finished 2004 |
> | NGS | fragment, add adapters, amplify on a surface, sequence by synthesis, millions at once |
> | Pyrosequencing | one dNTP at a time; pyrophosphate → ATP → luciferase flash; 400–500 nt reads |
> | Reversible terminators | fluorescent, blocked dNTPs: one base per cycle, image, unblock (Illumina) |
> | Uses | de novo genome sequencing, targeted re-sequencing, transcriptome profiling |
