# Diseases from defective DNA repair

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[DNA Damage and Repair (Lecture 15)]]

*Source: Lecture 15: DNA damage and repair (PowerPoint), slides 22*

| Syndrome | Affected mechanism | Major cancer predisposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ataxia-telangiectasia (AT)** | DSB response/repair | lymphomas |
| **Nijmegen breakage syndrome (NBS)** | DSB response/repair | lymphomas |
| **BRCA1/BRCA2** | HR | breast, ovarian cancer |
| **Bloom syndrome (BLM)** | HR | leukaemia, lymphoma, others |
| **Werner syndrome (WRN)** | HR | various cancers |
| **[[Hereditary non-polyposis colorectal cancer]] (HNPCC)** | MMR | colorectal cancer |
| **Turcot syndrome** | MMR | medulloblastoma, glioma, lymphoma, colon cancer |
| **[[Xeroderma pigmentosum]] (XP)** | NER | UV-induced skin cancer |
| **Cockayne syndrome (CS)** | NER | **no** cancer predisposition; photosensitivity, physical and mental retardation |
| **Trichothiodystrophy (TTD)** | NER | **no** cancer predisposition; photosensitivity, physical and mental retardation |

- Faulty repair lets mutations build up, so most of these raise the risk of **cancer**
- XP patients cannot repair UV damage, so sunlight causes skin cancers

> [!quote] Klug 12e, p. front matter
> Xeroderma pigmentosum is a rare recessive disorder in which affected individuals are highly sensitive to UV radiation and have a 2000-fold higher incidence of cancer than unaffected individuals.

## Questions
> [!question]- Which repair pathway is defective in xeroderma pigmentosum, and what is the result?
> Nucleotide excision repair; UV-induced skin cancer.

> [!question]- Which repair pathway is defective in HNPCC?
> Mismatch repair; it causes colorectal cancer.

> [!question]- Which repair pathway do BRCA1 and BRCA2 work in?
> Homologous recombination (double strand break repair); defects predispose to breast and ovarian cancer.

> [!question]- Name two NER defect syndromes with no cancer predisposition.
> Cockayne syndrome and trichothiodystrophy (photosensitivity, physical and mental retardation).
