> [!key] At a glance

> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | Endogenous damage | from inside the cell: oxygen radicals, replication errors |
> | Exogenous damage | from outside: ionising radiation, UV, environmental chemicals |
> | UV damage | pyrimidine dimers (cyclobutane dimer, 6-4 photoproduct) that kink the helix |
> | Photoreactivation | photolyase + blue light splits pyrimidine dimers; one protein; not in placental mammals |
> | NER | cut out a stretch around a bulky lesion: 13 nt in E. coli, 29 nt in humans |
> | MMR | MutS finds the mismatch, MutL joins, MutH cuts the unmethylated new strand at GATC |
> | XP | defective NER: UV-induced skin cancer |
> | HNPCC | defective MMR: colorectal cancer |
