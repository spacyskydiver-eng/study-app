# Summary (DNA Damage and Repair)

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[DNA Damage and Repair (Lecture 15)]]

*Source: Lecture 15: DNA damage and repair (PowerPoint), slides 23*

- DNA damage can be **exogenous** (ionising radiation, UV) or **endogenous** (oxygen radicals, replication errors)
- **Damage**:
  - Oxygen radicals add hydroxyl and carbonyl groups to bases and can break the DNA backbone
  - UV crosslinks pyrimidine bases, forming **pyrimidine dimers**
  - Replication errors cause **mismatches, insertions and deletions**
- **Repair**:
  - **Photoreactivation** directly repairs pyrimidine dimers
  - **MMR, NER and BER** remove damaged nucleotides and replace them using the undamaged strand as a template
- Defects in human DNA repair cause disease, including **cancers**
