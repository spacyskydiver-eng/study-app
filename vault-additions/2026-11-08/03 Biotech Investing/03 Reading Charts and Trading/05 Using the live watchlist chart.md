# Using the live watchlist chart

> [!path] [[Biotech Investing]] › [[Reading Charts and Trading]]

Open it from [[Biotech Investing]] or the navigator: **Live watchlist**.

## The cards
- Price, today's change (green up, red down), a **live** label while the US market is open
- The bar under the price is the **[[52-week range]]**: the dot shows where today's price sits between the year's low and high
- Programme, next catalyst and **[[Cash runway]]** come from the company notes. Tap a card to chart that company

## The chart controls
| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **5m / 1H / 1D / 1W** | the [[Timeframe]] of each bar |
| **1M … 5Y, All** | how much history fits on screen (daily and weekly only) |
| **Candles / Line** | [[Candlestick]] or line chart |
| **MA** | the 50 and 200 bar [[Moving average|moving averages]] |
| **Key event / Trial / Paper / News** | which markers show on the chart |
| Drag, scroll or pinch, double-click | move through time, zoom, reset |

## The markers
| Marker | Meaning | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **K** (orange arrow) | key event: results, FDA decisions, big readouts | researched list plus the company notes |
| **T** (blue circle) | trial started, changed status or posted results | ClinicalTrials.gov |
| **P** (green square) | a new paper on the company's drugs | PubMed |
| **N** (grey circle) | a news story | news feeds |
| a number | that many events on that bar | |

- Tap a marker to see what happened. The **share price around this** figure compares the close before the event with the close about a week later. It's the market's verdict on the news
- Events on weekends sit on the next trading day's candle, which is the first day the price could react

## The lists
- **Coming up**: dated future events (FDA dates, conferences, estimated trial completion dates) and what each company has guided
- **Updates**: everything, newest first, filterable by company and type. Tap the ticker to jump the chart to that day
- **Sources**: which automatic checks are working

## A 10-minute weekly routine
1. **Coming up**: anything in the next month? Note the date and what a good and a bad result would look like (see [[08 Event playbook for biotech shares|Event playbook for biotech shares]])
2. **Updates** filtered to Key event and Trial: anything new? Read the one or two that matter, not the whole feed
3. For each new key event, tap its marker: how did the price react? Did it match what you expected? Write one line in the trading journal ([[Trading journal]])
4. Glance at each card's 52-week dot and runway: is anyone near a low *and* running short of cash? That's where a share sale (dilution) is likely

## Questions
> [!question]- What does the K marker on the watchlist chart mean?
> A key event such as results, an FDA decision or a big trial readout.

> [!question]- Where does the watchlist get T markers from?
> ClinicalTrials.gov: trial starts, status changes and posted results.

> [!question]- What does the "share price around this" figure measure?
> The change from the close before the event to the close about a week after it: the market's reaction.

> [!question]- Why are weekend events placed on Monday's candle?
> Monday is the first session in which the price can react to them.

> [!question]- What combination on a watchlist card hints that a share sale may be coming?
> A price near its 52-week low together with a short cash runway.
