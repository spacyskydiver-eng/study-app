# Event playbook for biotech shares

> [!path] [[Biotech Investing]] › [[Reading Charts and Trading]]

## Before the event (days to weeks ahead)
1. **What exactly is being measured?** Primary endpoint, comparator, number of patients, duration
2. **What counts as good and bad?** Write numbers down before the result: e.g. "a placebo-adjusted sweat chloride drop of 10+ mmol/L is good, under 5 is a failure"
3. **What's priced in?** Look at the chart: a strong run-up means high hopes and a risk of [[Sell the news]]
4. **How much cash?** Under about 2 years of [[Cash runway]] → expect a share sale soon after any good news
5. **What's left if it fails?** Cash per share and other programmes set the floor

## Three ways to play it
| Approach | What you do | Upside | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sit out** | no position; watch and learn | none | none; you still learn from the reaction |
| **Small bet before** | a position sized so a 90% loss is fine ([[Position sizing]]) | full move if it works | can lose most of it overnight |
| **Wait and buy after** | read the full data, buy only if the market under-reacted | smaller, but with far more information | can miss the jump |

- A [[Stop-loss order]] does not limit a loss on a binary event because of overnight gaps. The only real protection is a small position
- For learning, "wait and buy after" plus "sit out" teaches the most for the least money

## After the event
- Read the **full** release, not just the headline. Check the numbers against what you wrote down
- Don't chase a gap in the first minutes; spreads are wide and prices swing. Wait for the first hour or the next day
- Watch for a share sale in the following days
- After a big drop, compare **[[Market capitalisation]] with cash**. If the company is worth less than its cash, the market is saying it expects that cash to be spent on failures. That can be a value opportunity or a trap ("falling knife")

## Worked examples from the watchlist
**Sionna, 10 August 2026**
- Set-up: SION-719 add-on trial, 15 F508del-homozygous adults already on Trikafta; shares had traded above $44 in July
- Result: −1.0 mmol/L placebo-adjusted sweat chloride (p=0.7), a clear miss. Shares about −90%
- Lesson: a tiny add-on trial against the best drug was a high bar; the price before the event assumed success. A small position (or none) was the only protection

**4DMT, 6 November 2025**
- Positive long-term 4D-150 data and a $100M share sale at $10.51 on the same day
- Lesson: good news and dilution often arrive together. Holders get the data and a smaller slice of the company

**Krystal, 2 November 2026**
- Revenue beat expectations, but KB407 and KB803 timelines slipped to 2027, and the shares fell
- Lesson: for investors, timelines are part of the news. Read past the headline number

## Questions
> [!question]- What five things should you check before a biotech catalyst?
> What is measured, what counts as good or bad (numbers written down first), what is priced in (run-up), cash runway (likely share sale) and what is left if it fails.

> [!question]- Why write down good and bad thresholds before a result?
> So you judge the result against your own expectations rather than the company's spin or the price reaction.

> [!question]- What is the only real protection against a failed readout?
> A small position size; stop-losses gap through overnight.

> [!question]- What does a market cap below the company's cash suggest?
> The market expects much of the cash to be spent without success; it may be a value opportunity or a trap.

> [!question]- What lesson does Krystal's 2 November 2026 reaction teach?
> Timelines are part of the news: a revenue beat can still mean a falling share price if programmes are delayed.

> [!question]- What are the three ways to approach a binary event?
> Sit out, take a small position before it, or wait and buy after the data.
