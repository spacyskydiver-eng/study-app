# Reading a price chart

> [!path] [[Biotech Investing]] › [[Reading Charts and Trading]]

## What is on the screen
| Part | Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Price axis** | right edge | the share price; on the watchlist it's in US dollars |
| **Time axis** | bottom | the date or time of each bar |
| **Last price line** | dotted line with a coloured label on the right | the most recent trade price |
| **Bars or candles** | the main area | the price over each time period |
| **Volume bars** | along the bottom | how many shares changed hands in each period |
| **Readout** | top left of the watchlist chart | open, high, low, close and volume of the bar under the cursor |

## Market hours (US shares, UK time)
| Session | UK time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-market | 9:00 to 14:30 | thin trading, big spreads; many apps don't offer it |
| **Regular session** | **14:30 to 21:00** | almost all the volume; the "live" label shows on the watchlist |
| After-hours | 21:00 to 01:00 | where results released after the close first move the price |
| Weekends and US holidays | closed | no new bars; a chart's last bar is the last trading day |

- The UK and US change clocks on different weekends, so for about two weeks in March and one in late October/November the session is 13:30 to 20:00
- Companies usually release big news **before the open** (around 11:00 to 13:00 UK) or **after the close** (21:00+), so the price can't react until the next session. That's why news shows up as a **[[Price gap]]**

## Line chart or candles
- **Line chart**: joins each period's closing price. Clean, good for the big picture over years
- **[[Candlestick]] chart**: each period shows four prices ([[OHLC]]), so you see how the price moved *within* the period as well. Traders use candles for anything from days to months

## The time setting ([[Timeframe]])
- Each bar is one period: **5m** (5 minutes), **1H** (an hour), **1D** (a day), **1W** (a week)
- The same move looks very different by timeframe: one daily candle contains 78 five-minute candles (6.5 hours × 12)
- Rule of thumb: pick the timeframe that matches how long you plan to hold. Holding for months → daily and weekly. The 5-minute chart is mostly noise for that
- Zoom (1M, 3M, 1Y…) only changes how much history is on screen, not the size of each bar

## Questions
> [!question]- What are the regular trading hours of US shares in UK time (most of the year)?
> 14:30 to 21:00.

> [!question]- Why does big company news usually appear as a gap on the chart?
> It is released before the open or after the close, so the first trade after the news happens at a very different price from the last one before it.

> [!question]- What does a line chart plot?
> Only the closing price of each period, joined up.

> [!question]- How many 5-minute candles make one daily candle for a US share?
> 78 (6.5 hours × 12 per hour).

> [!question]- What is the difference between changing the timeframe and changing the zoom?
> The timeframe sets how long each bar covers (5 minutes, a day, a week); the zoom only sets how much history is shown.
