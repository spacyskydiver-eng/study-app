# Krystal Biotech (KRYS)

> [!path] [[Biotech Investing]] › [[Cystic Fibrosis Pipeline]]

- **Listing**: Nasdaq, KRYS. Mid cap and **profitable**
- Already sells **Vyjuvek**, a topical HSV-1 gene therapy for the skin disease dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa: **$127 million** revenue in Q3 2026 (up 30%), net income $63 million, $1.2 billion cash
- **KB407**: a **nebulised, non-replicating HSV-1 vector** carrying two copies of the full CFTR gene. HSV-1 can carry large genes and can be redosed

## Data and plans
- CORAL-1 Phase 1: at the highest dose (four daily doses of 10⁹ PFU), **29 to 42%** of conducting airway cells were transduced in biopsies, with CFTR protein at the cell surface; no serious treatment-related safety problems
- Interim results from the current study now **H1 2027**; registrational study planned for **2027**, pending FDA alignment (timelines slipped from 2026)

## Investing view
- Profitable, so a CF failure would hurt the price but not threaten the company: a lower-risk way to get exposure to CF gene therapy
