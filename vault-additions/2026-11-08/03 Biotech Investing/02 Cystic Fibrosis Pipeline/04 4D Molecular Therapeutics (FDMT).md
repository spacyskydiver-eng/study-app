# 4D Molecular Therapeutics (FDMT)

> [!path] [[Biotech Investing]] › [[Cystic Fibrosis Pipeline]]

- **Listing**: Nasdaq, FDMT. Small to mid cap
- **4D-710**: an **inhaled [[AAV vector|AAV]]** carrying a CFTR gene (a shortened version that fits inside AAV), using an engineered capsid chosen for airway cells
- Target group: people with CF who **can't take or don't respond to modulators**

## Data and plans
- Phase 1 (AEROW): high CFTR expression across airway biopsies, well above normal lung levels
- **October 2025**: moved into Phase 2 at 2.5 × 10¹⁴ vector genomes (the expected pivotal dose); a **redosing** cohort gives a second dose after at least a year
- CF Foundation investing up to **$11 million** in 4DMT shares
- Endpoints include [[ppFEV1]], lung clearance index (LCI2.5) and CT scans
- **Next catalyst**: AEROW Phase 1/2 data expected **H2 2026**

## Money
- About $431 million cash and securities (30 June 2026), net loss about $73 million a quarter; the company guides runway into **H2 2028**
- Most of the company's value is its **eye disease** drug 4D-150 (Phase 3, partnered with Otsuka in Asia), so CF is not the main driver of the share price

## Questions
> [!question]- What is 4D-710?
> An inhaled AAV gene therapy delivering a CFTR gene to airway cells, for people with CF who can’t use modulators.

> [!question]- Why does 4DMT run a redosing cohort?
> Airway cells turn over, so expression may fade; it tests whether a second dose works and is safe despite immunity to AAV.

> [!question]- Why might CF news not move FDMT shares much?
> Most of its value is in its eye disease drug 4D-150, which is in Phase 3.
