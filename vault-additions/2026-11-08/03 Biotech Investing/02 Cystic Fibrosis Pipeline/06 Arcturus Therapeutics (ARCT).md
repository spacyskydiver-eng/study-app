# Arcturus Therapeutics (ARCT)

> [!path] [[Biotech Investing]] › [[Cystic Fibrosis Pipeline]]

- **Listing**: Nasdaq, ARCT. Small cap
- **ARCT-032**: CFTR **mRNA** in an inhaled **[[Lipid nanoparticle|lipid nanoparticle]]**. The cell makes CFTR protein from the mRNA; no DNA enters the nucleus
- Target: people with **class I** mutations (no CFTR protein)

## Data and plans
- Interim Phase 2 data (2025) showed early signals of mucus reduction but were widely described as **"mixed"**, and the shares fell sharply
- 2026: a **12-week Phase 2** study, up to 20 people with class I mutations, 10 mg daily (up to 15 mg), with tighter entry criteria to reduce variability
- Cost cuts extend runway **into 2028**

## Investing view
- mRNA has to be given repeatedly (it's temporary), so long-term tolerability is the key question, the same problem that ended Vertex's VX-522

## Questions
> [!question]- How does ARCT-032 differ from a gene therapy like 4D-710?
> It delivers mRNA in lipid nanoparticles, so cells make CFTR protein temporarily without DNA entering the nucleus; it must be given repeatedly.

> [!question]- Why did Arcturus shares fall on its 2025 CF data?
> The interim Phase 2 data were mixed: hints of mucus reduction but no clear lung function benefit.
