# Using this for the seminar essay and presentation

> [!path] [[Biotech Investing]] › [[Cystic Fibrosis Pipeline]]

Group 4's topic is **future therapies for CF** ([[09 Presentation topics|presentation topics]]), presented in seminar 6. The essay (1500 words, due 10 December; plan due 16 November) has the same title as the series: *From Bench to Bedside: The Role of Experimental Models in Cystic Fibrosis Drug Development* (see [[01 The seminar series and its assessments|assessments]]).

## What carries over
- **Bench to bedside in action**: each programme went through the models in the seminars:
  - **cell and [[07 Organoids as pre-clinical models|organoid]] models** to show CFTR function is restored
  - **animal models** ([[06 Modelling cystic fibrosis in vivo, CF pigs|CF pigs]], ferrets) to test lung delivery
  - **Phase 1 biopsies** showing CFTR in human airway cells (4D-710, KB407)
- **Why models can mislead**: SION-719 worked in nonclinical models but showed no effect in patients; BI 3720931 and VX-522 failed in people. Good material for a critical paragraph on the limits of models
- **Unmet need**: about 10% of people with CF have mutations modulators can't help; that's the case for genetic therapies

## Presentation structure (from [[02 What makes an effective scientific presentation|Seminar 2]])
1. Background: CFTR, mutation classes, what modulators can't fix
2. Why it matters: the class I gap
3. Two to four key papers or trials, with the experimental detail: vector, model, outcome
4. Conclusion: what's promising, what failed, what's next

## Sources
- Press releases and company results are **not peer-reviewed**: fine for current status, but the essay's reflective commentary needs published primary research or review articles
- A strong starting review: *Inhalable gene and RNA therapy for cystic fibrosis: perspectives and progress in clinical development*, Nanomedicine (2026), doi:10.1080/17435889.2026.2640157
- Trial records on ClinicalTrials.gov: 4D-710 AEROW NCT05248230; KB407 CORAL-1 NCT05504837; VX-522 NCT05668741

## Questions
> [!question]- Why are company press releases not enough as essay sources?
> They are not peer-reviewed; the essay and reflective commentary need published primary research or review articles.

> [!question]- What fraction of people with CF can’t benefit from modulators, and why does that matter for Group 4’s topic?
> About 10%, mostly with class I (nonsense) mutations; it is the main case for genetic therapies.

> [!question]- Give an example where a CF drug looked good in models but failed in patients.
> SION-719 (no sweat chloride effect in Phase 2a), or the stopped VX-522 and BI 3720931 genetic therapies.
