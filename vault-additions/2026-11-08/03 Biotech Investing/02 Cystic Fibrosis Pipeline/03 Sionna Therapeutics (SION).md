# Sionna Therapeutics (SION)

> [!path] [[Biotech Investing]] › [[Cystic Fibrosis Pipeline]]

- **Listing**: Nasdaq, SION (listed 2025). Small cap
- **Idea**: [[F508del]] destabilises the **NBD1 domain** of CFTR. Current correctors don't fully fix NBD1; an **[[NBD1 stabiliser]]** might get CFTR function close to normal

## Timeline
- April 2026: SION-719 Phase 2a (add-on to Trikafta) fully enrolled
- **10 August 2026**: SION-719 missed (sweat chloride −1.0 mmol/L vs placebo, p = 0.7; 15 adults, 14 days, crossover). Dual combination SION-451 + SION-2222 (galicaftor) passed its Phase 1 safety goals
- September 2026: 46% of staff cut; runway extended into **second half of 2029**
- **Next catalyst**: AscenSION CF Phase 2a of SION-451 + SION-2222 to start **Q1 2027**

## Lessons
- Strong preclinical science ≠ benefit in patients
- A 14-day, 15-patient trial on top of an already very effective drug had little room to show anything
- Cash ($268 million) means it survives to try again; the question is whether a dual combination can compete with Trikafta and Alyftrek

## Questions
> [!question]- What is Sionna’s scientific idea for treating F508del CF?
> Stabilise the NBD1 domain of CFTR, which F508del destabilises, to restore near-normal CFTR function.

> [!question]- What was the result of the SION-719 Phase 2a trial?
> A placebo-adjusted sweat chloride change of −1.0 mmol/L (p = 0.7) on top of Trikafta: no meaningful effect.

> [!question]- What is Sionna’s next CF trial and when?
> A Phase 2a (AscenSION CF) of the SION-451 + SION-2222 dual combination, planned to start in Q1 2027.
