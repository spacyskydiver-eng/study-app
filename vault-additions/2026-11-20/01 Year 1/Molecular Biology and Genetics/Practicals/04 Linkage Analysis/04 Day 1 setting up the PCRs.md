# Day 1: setting up the PCRs

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage Analysis practical]]

*Source: Practical Manual 2 4018A (Genetics and transformation), page 7*

## Two separate tubes
- **M1 PCR**: primers **M1F + M1R**, amplifies M1a and M1b
- **M2 PCR**: primers **M2F + M2R**, amplifies M2a and M2b
- The **PCR Master Mix** is already in the thin-walled tube (**red dot, 'MM'**)

| Constituent | Label | Volume (µL) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PCR Master Mix (already in tube) | 'MM', red dot | 25 |
| Nuclease-free water | 'NFW', blue dot | 13 |
| Forward primer (4 µM) | '1F' or '2F', white dot | 5 |
| Reverse primer (4 µM) | '1R' or '2R', white dot | 5 |
| Progeny DNA | 'DNA', yellow dot | 2 |
| **Total** | | **50** |

- Pipette up and down **2–3 times** to mix, cap and **label** the tubes, and briefly **spin** out any bubbles

> [!example] Worked: final primer concentration
> 5 µL of 4 µM primer in a 50 µL reaction: C₂ = C₁V₁ / V₂ = 4 × 5 / 50 = **0.4 µM** of each primer.

## Thermal cycling

| Step | Temperature | Time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 94 °C | 5 min | Initial **[[Denaturation|denaturation]]** of the template |
| 2 | 94 °C | 30 s | Denature: strands separate |
| 3 | 55 °C | 30 s | **[[Annealing]]**: primers bind |
| 4 | 72 °C | 60 s | **Extension**: Taq polymerase copies |
| 5 | Back to step 2 | 30 cycles in total | |
| 6 | 72 °C | 10 min | Final extension: finishes all products |

## Questions
> [!question]- What volumes go into each PCR tube, and what is the total?
> 25 µL master mix, 13 µL nuclease-free water, 5 µL forward primer, 5 µL reverse primer and 2 µL DNA: 50 µL.

> [!question]- What is the final concentration of each primer in the reaction?
> 5 µL × 4 µM / 50 µL = 0.4 µM.

> [!question]- What happens at 94 °C, 55 °C and 72 °C in each cycle?
> 94 °C denatures the DNA into single strands, 55 °C lets the primers anneal, 72 °C lets the polymerase extend them.

> [!question]- Why are M1 and M2 amplified in separate tubes?
> Each tube has its own primer pair, so each lane on the gel shows only one marker's alleles and the bands can't be confused.
