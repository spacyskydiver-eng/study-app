# The cross and the four progeny genotypes

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage Analysis practical]]

*Source: Practical Manual 2 4018A (Genetics and transformation), pages 5-6, Figures 2 and 3*

## The cross
- **Parent 1**: heterozygous at both markers, **M1a/M1b, M2a/M2b**. M1a and M2a are on one chromosome, M1b and M2b on the other
- **Parent 2**: homozygous for the **a** alleles, **M1a/M1a, M2a/M2a**
- This is a **[[Test cross|test cross]]**: Parent 2 can only give an **M1a–M2a** gamete, so the progeny's **b** alleles must all come from Parent 1

> [!figure] The cross: Parent 1 carries M1a–M2a and M1b–M2b; Parent 2 only M1a–M2a
> ![[mg_p4_cross.svg]]

## Parent 1's gametes
- **Parental ([[Non-recombinant|non-recombinant]])**: **M1a–M2a** or **M1b–M2b**, the combinations Parent 1 already had
- **[[Recombinant]]**: **M1a–M2b** or **M1b–M2a**, made only when a crossover happens between M1 and M2

## The four progeny genotypes

| Progeny | Genotype | M1 bands | M2 bands | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| i | M1a/M1a, M2a/M2a | 521 | 1020 | Non-recombinant |
| ii | M1a/M1b, M2a/M2b | 521 + 761 | 1020 + 1500 | Non-recombinant |
| iii | M1a/M1a, M2a/M2b | 521 | 1020 + 1500 | **Recombinant** |
| iv | M1a/M1b, M2a/M2a | 521 + 761 | 1020 | **Recombinant** |

> [!tip] Quick rule
> **Both markers the same** (both one band, or both two bands) = **non-recombinant**. **One marker heterozygous and the other not** = **recombinant**.

## Questions
> [!question]- Why is crossing the double heterozygote with an M1a/M1a M2a/M2a mouse useful?
> It is a test cross: Parent 2 only gives a and a alleles, so each progeny genotype shows exactly which gamete Parent 1 made, recombinant or not.

> [!question]- Which two gametes from Parent 1 are recombinant?
> M1a–M2b and M1b–M2a.

> [!question]- A progeny mouse shows one band (521 bp) for M1 and two bands (1020 and 1500 bp) for M2. Genotype, and is it recombinant?
> M1a/M1a, M2a/M2b. Recombinant: Parent 1 gave an M1a–M2b gamete.

> [!question]- A progeny mouse shows 521 + 761 bp for M1 and 1020 + 1500 bp for M2. Genotype, and is it recombinant?
> M1a/M1b, M2a/M2b. Non-recombinant: Parent 1 gave its parental M1b–M2b gamete.
