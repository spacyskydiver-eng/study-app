# Day 2: running the gel

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage Analysis practical]]

*Source: Practical Manual 2 4018A (Genetics and transformation), page 8*

## Steps
1. Mix **10 µL PCR product** with **2.5 µL 5X loading buffer** ('LD')
2. Load your samples, **one per lane**. A demonstrator loads **5 µL of the 1 kb ladder** ('1Kb')
3. Run the **[[Gel electrophoresis|agarose gel]]**, judging progress by how far the loading dye has moved
4. View on a **transilluminator** and photograph the gel

> [!warning] Safety
> The transilluminator gives out **UV**: use the shield and eye and skin protection.

> [!example] Worked: why 2.5 µL of 5X buffer?
> Total volume 10 + 2.5 = 12.5 µL. 5X × 2.5 / 12.5 = **1X**, the working strength.

## Reading it
- Compare your bands with the **1 kb ladder** to estimate sizes, then match them to Table 1: 521 / 761 for M1, 1020 / 1500 for M2
- Smaller fragments run **further** from the wells

## Questions
> [!question]- How much loading buffer is added to 10 µL of PCR product, and what final strength does that give?
> 2.5 µL of 5X buffer: 12.5 µL in total at 1X.

> [!question]- Why is a 1 kb ladder run on the gel?
> To give bands of known size, so the size of the PCR products can be estimated.

> [!question]- What safety precaution is needed when viewing the gel?
> The transilluminator gives out UV light, so use the shield and protect your eyes and skin.
