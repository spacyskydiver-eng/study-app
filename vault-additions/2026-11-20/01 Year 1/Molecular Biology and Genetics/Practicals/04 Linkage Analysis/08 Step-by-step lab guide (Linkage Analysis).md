# Step-by-step lab guide

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage Analysis practical]]

*Source: Practical Manual 2 4018A (Genetics and transformation), pages 7-8. Volumes, labels and temperatures are from the manual; pipette choices and handling tips are standard lab practice, so follow a demonstrator if your bench is set up differently.*

> [!abstract] What you'll make
> **Day 1:** two PCR tubes from your one progeny DNA: **M1 tube** (primers 1F + 1R) and **M2 tube** (primers 2F + 2R), 50 µL each.
> **Day 2:** run both on a gel, read the bands, work out your mouse's genotype, put it on the class spreadsheet.

## Before you start
- [ ] Lab coat on (required for every session), gloves on
- [ ] Find your reagents. Each tube has a **coloured dot**:

| Tube | Dot | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MM** | 🔴 red | PCR Master Mix, **25 µL already inside** a thin-walled PCR tube. You get **two** of these: one becomes M1, one becomes M2 |
| **NFW** | 🔵 blue | Nuclease-free water |
| **1F** / **1R** | ⚪ white | M1 forward and reverse primers (4 µM) |
| **2F** / **2R** | ⚪ white | M2 forward and reverse primers (4 µM) |
| **DNA** | 🟡 yellow | Your progeny mouse's DNA |

- [ ] Find your pipettes. You need:
  - **P20** (2–20 µL, usually yellow button): for 13 µL and 5 µL
  - **P10** or **P2** (if there is one): for the 2 µL of DNA. A P20 also works at 2 µL but is least accurate at the bottom of its range
  - Fresh **tips** that fit each pipette (small/white or yellow tips for P10/P20)

> [!tip] Pipetting in 5 seconds
> 1. Turn the dial to the volume (P20 reading **1 3 0** = 13.0 µL; **0 5 0** = 5.0 µL; **0 2 0** = 2.0 µL)
> 2. Push a **new tip** on firmly
> 3. Press the plunger to the **first stop**, put the tip just under the liquid, **slowly** let it up
> 4. Put the tip into the liquid already in the PCR tube, press to the **first stop**, then the **second stop** to push out the last drop
> 5. Keep the plunger pressed while you lift out, then eject the tip into the waste
> **New tip for every reagent and every tube.**

## Day 1: set up the two PCRs

### Label first
- [ ] Take the **two red-dot MM tubes**
- [ ] On the **lid and the side** write **M1 + your initials** on one, **M2 + your initials** on the other (thin-walled tubes are small, so keep it short)

### M1 tube (add in this order)
| Step | Pipette | Dial | Take from | Put into |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | P20 | **13 µL** | 🔵 **NFW** | **M1** tube |
| 2 | P20 | **5 µL** | ⚪ **1F** | **M1** tube |
| 3 | P20 | **5 µL** | ⚪ **1R** | **M1** tube |
| 4 | P10 / P2 | **2 µL** | 🟡 **DNA** | **M1** tube |

- [ ] Pipette gently **up and down 2–3 times** to mix (with the tip from step 4, set to about 20 µL on a P20 if you want a bigger mix; don't make bubbles)
- [ ] Close the lid firmly

### M2 tube (same again, with the M2 primers)
| Step | Pipette | Dial | Take from | Put into |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | P20 | **13 µL** | 🔵 **NFW** | **M2** tube |
| 6 | P20 | **5 µL** | ⚪ **2F** | **M2** tube |
| 7 | P20 | **5 µL** | ⚪ **2R** | **M2** tube |
| 8 | P10 / P2 | **2 µL** | 🟡 **DNA** | **M2** tube |

- [ ] Mix **up and down 2–3 times**, close the lid

> [!warning] Don't mix up the primers
> **1F and 1R only go in M1. 2F and 2R only go in M2.** If you put 2F in the M1 tube you'll get the wrong bands and a wrong genotype.

### Check and spin
- [ ] Each tube now holds **25 + 13 + 5 + 5 + 2 = 50 µL**. Both should look about the same height
- [ ] Any **bubbles**? Put both tubes in the **microcentrifuge**, balanced opposite each other, and spin for a few seconds

### Into the PCR machine
- [ ] Put both tubes into the **thermal cycler**. Note which **slot numbers** you used
- [ ] The program (usually already set by the demonstrators):

| Step | Temperature | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 94 °C | 5 min |
| 2 | 94 °C | 30 s |
| 3 | 55 °C | 30 s |
| 4 | 72 °C | 60 s |
| 5 | Repeat steps 2–4 | 30 cycles in total |
| 6 | 72 °C | 10 min |

- [ ] Write in your manual: tube labels, slot numbers, date

## Day 2: run the gel

### Mix with loading buffer
- [ ] Get your **M1** and **M2** PCR tubes back
- [ ] For **each** tube, in a fresh tube (or as shown by the demonstrator):

| Step | Pipette | Dial | Take from | Put into |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | P20 | **10 µL** | your **M1** PCR | new tube labelled **M1 load** |
| 2 | P10 / P20 | **2.5 µL** | **LD** (5X loading buffer) | **M1 load** |
| 3 | P20 | **10 µL** | your **M2** PCR | new tube labelled **M2 load** |
| 4 | P10 / P20 | **2.5 µL** | **LD** | **M2 load** |

- [ ] Mix each by pipetting **up and down** a couple of times. Each now has **12.5 µL at 1X** loading buffer

### Load
- [ ] Find your **lanes** on the shared gel and **write down the lane order**, e.g. lane 1 = 1 kb ladder, lane 4 = your M1, lane 5 = your M2
- [ ] A **demonstrator loads 5 µL of the 1 kb ladder ('1Kb')**
- [ ] P20 set to **12.5 µL**: take up all of **M1 load**, put the tip **just inside the top of the well** (not through the bottom), push out **slowly to the first stop only** so no air bubble goes in. The blue sample sinks into the well
- [ ] New tip, same for **M2 load** in the next lane
- [ ] Ask a demonstrator to show you if you haven't loaded a gel before

### Run and view
- [ ] Gel runs until the **blue dye front** has moved far enough down (demonstrators decide)
- [ ] View on the **transilluminator**. **UV light**: close the shield / wear the face guard, no bare skin under it
- [ ] **Photograph** the gel

## Day 2: work out your genotype
- [ ] **M1 lane**: one band at **~521 bp** = **M1a/M1a**; bands at **521 and 761 bp** = **M1a/M1b**
- [ ] **M2 lane**: one band at **~1020 bp** = **M2a/M2a**; bands at **1020 and 1500 bp** = **M2a/M2b**
- [ ] Use the **1 kb ladder** to judge sizes (bands near 500, 750, 1000 and 1500)
- [ ] Match to the table:

| Your M1 | Your M2 | Genotype | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 band | 1 band | **i** M1a/M1a, M2a/M2a | Non-recombinant |
| 2 bands | 2 bands | **ii** M1a/M1b, M2a/M2b | Non-recombinant |
| 1 band | 2 bands | **iii** M1a/M1a, M2a/M2b | **Recombinant** |
| 2 bands | 1 band | **iv** M1a/M1b, M2a/M2a | **Recombinant** |

- [ ] Enter your genotype on the **shared class spreadsheet**
- [ ] When everyone's in: **RF = recombinants (iii + iv) ÷ total progeny**, then **× 100 = distance in cM** (see [[06 Calculating recombination frequency and map distance|the calculation]])

> [!example] Fill-in for your manual
> My mouse: M1 bands ______ M2 bands ______ → genotype **___** (recombinant / non-recombinant)
> Class: ___ recombinant out of ___ total → RF = ____ → M1–M2 distance = ____ cM

## If something goes wrong
- **No bands in a lane**: PCR failed (missing DNA or primer, or wrong slot) or the sample floated out of the well. Tell a demonstrator
- **Band at the wrong size**: check you used the right primers in that tube
- **Bands blurred or smeared**: too much sample or the gel ran too fast. The genotype can usually still be read
