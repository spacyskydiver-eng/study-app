# The dihybrid cross

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Mendelian Inheritance - Independent Assortment (Lecture 18)]]

*Source: Lecture 18: Mendelian inheritance - independent assortment (PowerPoint), slides 3-8*

## Two characters at once
- Stem length: **Le** (tall) dominant to **le** (dwarf)
- Seed colour: **I** (yellow) dominant to **i** (green)

| Generation | Genotype | Phenotype |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P₁ | LeLe II × lele ii | tall yellow × dwarf green |
| F₁ | Lele Ii | **all tall yellow** |
| F₁ gametes | Le I, Le i, le I, le i | each **¼** |

## F₂ (F₁ self-fertilised)
- 4 × 4 **Punnett square** = 16 boxes
- Phenotypes: **9 tall yellow : 3 tall green : 3 dwarf yellow : 1 dwarf green**
- The **9 : 3 : 3 : 1** ratio is the signature of two genes assorting independently

## Mendel's genes
- The seven pea genes (A/a, I/i, Le/le, Fa/fa, Gp/gp, R/r, V/v, P/p) map to different chromosomes or far apart on the same one, which is why they behaved independently

## Questions
> [!question]- What gametes does a Lele Ii plant make, and in what proportions?
> Le I, Le i, le I and le i, each ¼.

> [!question]- What is the F₂ phenotype ratio from a dihybrid cross of two true-breeding parents?
> 9 : 3 : 3 : 1.

> [!question]- In tall yellow × dwarf green, what is the genotype of the dwarf green F₂ plants?
> lele ii.
