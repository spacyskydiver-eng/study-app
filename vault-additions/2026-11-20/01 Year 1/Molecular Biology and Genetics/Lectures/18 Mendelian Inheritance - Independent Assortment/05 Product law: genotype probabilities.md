# Product law: genotype probabilities

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Mendelian Inheritance - Independent Assortment (Lecture 18)]]

*Source: Lecture 18: Mendelian inheritance - independent assortment (PowerPoint), slides 28-30*

- P(genotype) = **P(genotype at locus 1) × P(genotype at locus 2)**
- From an Aa × Aa monohybrid cross: P(AA) = ¼, P(Aa) = ½, P(aa) = ¼

## Example: P(Lele II)
- P(Lele) = ½, P(II) = ¼
- **½ × ¼ = 1/8** (0.125)
- Check on the Punnett square: 2 of 16 boxes = 1/8

## Trihybrid example
- AA BB CC × aa bb cc → F₁ Aa Bb Cc → F₂
- P(Aa Bb Cc) = ½ × ½ × ½ = **1/8 = 0.125**
- Works for any number of independently assorting genes, with no 8 × 8 Punnett square

## Questions
> [!question]- What is the probability of an F₂ plant with genotype Lele II?
> ½ × ¼ = 1/8.

> [!question]- AA BB CC × aa bb cc; what is P(Aa Bb Cc) in the F₂?
> ½ × ½ × ½ = 0.125.

> [!question]- What is P(aa bb) in the F₂ of AaBb × AaBb?
> ¼ × ¼ = 1/16.
