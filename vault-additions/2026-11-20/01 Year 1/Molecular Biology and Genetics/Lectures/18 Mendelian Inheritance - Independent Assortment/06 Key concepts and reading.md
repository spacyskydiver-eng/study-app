# Key concepts and reading (Lecture 18)

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Mendelian Inheritance - Independent Assortment (Lecture 18)]]

*Source: Lecture 18: Mendelian inheritance - independent assortment (PowerPoint), slides 32-33*

- **Law of independent assortment**: each pair of alleles assorts independently of each other pair during gamete formation
- **Phenotype ratio**: a dihybrid cross between two true-breeding parents with two pairs of contrasting traits gives an F₂ of **9 : 3 : 3 : 1**
- **Probabilities and frequencies**: the product law gives phenotype and genotype probabilities and expected numbers
