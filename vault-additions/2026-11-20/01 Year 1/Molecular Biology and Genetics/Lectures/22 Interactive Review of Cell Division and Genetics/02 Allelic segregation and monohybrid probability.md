# Allelic segregation and monohybrid probability

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Interactive Review of Cell Division and Genetics (Lecture 22)]]

*Source: Lecture 22: Interactive review of cell division and genetics (PowerPoint), slides 13-19*

- **Law of segregation**: the two alleles for a heritable character **separate** during gamete formation and end up in **different gametes**
- Happens at **meiosis I** (Le and le go to different daughter cells, assuming no crossover between the gene and the centromere)
- A heterozygote makes **equal numbers** of R and r gametes

## Cattle coat colour
- R (red) dominant to r (white); Rr bull × Rr cow

| | R | r |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R** | RR red | Rr red |
| **r** | Rr red | rr white |

- P(red calf) = 3/4 = **0.75**

## Questions
> [!question]- When does allelic segregation occur?
> Meiosis I.

> [!question]- Rr × Rr cattle (R = red, dominant). Probability of a red calf?
> 0.75.
