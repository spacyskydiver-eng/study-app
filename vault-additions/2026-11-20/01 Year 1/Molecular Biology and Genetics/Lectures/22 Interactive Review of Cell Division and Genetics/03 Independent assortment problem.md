# Independent assortment problem

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Interactive Review of Cell Division and Genetics (Lecture 22)]]

*Source: Lecture 22: Interactive review of cell division and genetics (PowerPoint), slides 20-22*

- R (red) > r (white); **H (hornless) > h (horned)**; genes **unlinked**
- RrHh bull × RrHh cow
- Product law: P(red) × P(horned) = **¾ × ¼ = 3/16 = 0.1875**
- The 4 × 4 Punnett square gives 9 red hornless : 3 red horned : 3 white hornless : 1 white horned
- Independent assortment: the two chromosome pairs line up either way at meiosis I, giving RH, Rh, rH, rh equally

## Questions
> [!question]- RrHh × RrHh cattle, genes unlinked. P(red coat and horns)?
> ¾ × ¼ = 0.1875.

> [!question]- Same cross. P(white and hornless)?
> ¼ × ¾ = 0.1875.
