# Meiosis review

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Interactive Review of Cell Division and Genetics (Lecture 22)]]

*Source: Lecture 22: Interactive review of cell division and genetics (PowerPoint), slides 2-12*

| Stage | What happens | Human cell (chromosomes) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diploid cell | homologous pairs, e.g. Le and le | **46** |
| S phase (chromosome replication) | each chromosome becomes 2 sister chromatids | **46** (92 chromatids) |
| **Meiosis I** | **bivalents separate** into different daughter cells (reductional) | **23** each |
| **Meiosis II** | **centromeres divide**, sister chromatids move to different cells | **23** each, 4 haploid gametes |

- Count **chromosomes by centromeres**: replication doubles chromatids, not chromosomes
- **Prophase I** is the most interesting phase for geneticists: homologues pair and **non-sister chromatids recombine** (crossing over at chiasmata)

## Questions
> [!question]- What happens during meiosis I?
> Bivalents (homologous pairs) separate into different daughter cells.

> [!question]- What happens during meiosis II?
> Centromeres divide and sister chromatids move into different daughter cells.

> [!question]- A human cell undergoes meiosis. Give chromosome numbers in the diploid cell, after replication, after meiosis I and in each gamete.
> 46, 46, 23, 23.

> [!question]- Why is prophase I the key stage for geneticists?
> Crossing over between non-sister chromatids happens there, making recombinant chromosomes.
