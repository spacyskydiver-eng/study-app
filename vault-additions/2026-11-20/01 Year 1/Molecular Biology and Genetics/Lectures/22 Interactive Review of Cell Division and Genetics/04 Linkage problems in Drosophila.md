# Linkage problems in Drosophila

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Interactive Review of Cell Division and Genetics (Lecture 22)]]

*Source: Lecture 22: Interactive review of cell division and genetics (PowerPoint), slides 23-28*

## P₁: y w / y w female (yellow, white) × y⁺ w⁺ / Y male (grey, red)
- Progeny: **grey, red females** (y⁺ w⁺ / y w) + **yellow, white males** (y w / Y)
- Males get their X from the mother; females get the father's y⁺ w⁺ X

## F₁ cross: y⁺ w⁺ / y w female × y w / Y male
- Recombinant gametes from the female are **y⁺ w** and **y w⁺**
- So **grey-bodied, white-eyed** (and yellow-bodied, red-eyed) progeny come from recombinant gametes

| Progeny (♀ and ♂) | % |
| --- | --- |
| Yellow body, white eyes | 48.5 |
| Grey body, red eyes | 49.5 |
| Grey body, white eyes | 1.0 |
| Yellow body, red eyes | 1.0 |

- Recombinants = 1.0 + 1.0 = **2.0 %** → y–w = **2.0 cM**

## Questions
> [!question]- y w / y w female × y⁺ w⁺ / Y male: what are the progeny?
> Grey-bodied, red-eyed females and yellow-bodied, white-eyed males.

> [!question]- y⁺ w⁺ / y w female × y w / Y male: which phenotype comes from a recombinant gamete?
> Grey-bodied, white-eyed (or yellow-bodied, red-eyed).

> [!question]- Recombinant classes are 1.0 % and 1.0 %. What is the y–w distance?
> 2.0 cM.
