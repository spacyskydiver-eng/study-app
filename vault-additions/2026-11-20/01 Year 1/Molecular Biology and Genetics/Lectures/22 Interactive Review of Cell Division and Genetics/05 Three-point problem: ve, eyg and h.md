# Three-point problem: ve, eyg and h

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Interactive Review of Cell Division and Genetics (Lecture 22)]]

*Source: Lecture 22: Interactive review of cell division and genetics (PowerPoint), slides 29-37*

Drosophila genes on chromosome III, recessive mutants: **ve** (veinlet veins), **eyg** (eyes gone), **h** (hairy body). Triple heterozygous females × triple mutant males:

| Phenotype | Number | Class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ve eyg h | 325 | NCO |
| + + + | 338 | NCO |
| ve + + | 126 | SCO (ve–h) |
| + eyg h | 120 | SCO (ve–h) |
| + eyg + | 35 | SCO (h–eyg) |
| ve + h | 37 | SCO (h–eyg) |
| + + h | 10 | DCO |
| ve eyg + | 9 | DCO |
| **Total** | **1000** | |

- **NCO** = most progeny: 325 and 338 → parent is **ve eyg h / + + +**
- **DCO** = fewest: 10 and 9
- **SCO** = the rest: 126 + 120 and 35 + 37
- **Middle gene**: ve eyg h (NCO) vs ve eyg + (DCO) → **h** has swapped → order **ve – h – eyg**

## Distances (worked from the table)
- ve–h = (126 + 120 + 10 + 9) / 1000 = **26.5 cM**
- h–eyg = (35 + 37 + 10 + 9) / 1000 = **9.1 cM**
- ve–eyg = 26.5 + 9.1 = **35.6 cM**

## Interference (worked)
- Expected DCO = 0.265 × 0.091 = 0.0241; observed = 19/1000 = 0.019
- C = 0.019 / 0.0241 ≈ **0.79**; I = 1 − C ≈ **0.21** (positive interference)

## Questions
> [!question]- Which classes are the NCOs?
> The classes with 325 and 338 progeny.

> [!question]- Which classes are the DCOs?
> The classes with 10 and 9 progeny.

> [!question]- Which gene is in the middle?
> h (hairy body).

> [!question]- What is the distance between ve and h?
> (126 + 120 + 10 + 9) / 1000 = 26.5 cM.
