# Key concepts and reading (Lecture 20)

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage and Mapping Part 1 (Lecture 20)]]

*Source: Lecture 20: Linkage and mapping (Part 1) (PowerPoint), slides 53-54*

- **Linkage** can modify Mendelian phenotype and genotype ratios. Allelic **segregation** still applies, but assortment of allelic pairs is **not independent**
- **Incomplete linkage** results from **crossing over** during **prophase I**: pairing, breaking and joining of chromosomes in meiosis (= **recombination**)
- Three-point mapping and interference are covered in [[Linkage and Mapping Part 2 (Lecture 21)]]
