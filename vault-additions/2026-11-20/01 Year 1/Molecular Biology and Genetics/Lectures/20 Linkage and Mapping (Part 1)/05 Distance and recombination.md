# Distance and recombination

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage and Mapping Part 1 (Lecture 20)]]

*Source: Lecture 20: Linkage and mapping (Part 1) (PowerPoint), slides 23-24*

- **Small distance** between genes → **few recombinant**, many parental gametes (e.g. y–w, 1.3 %)
- As distance **increases**, the number of recombinant gametes **approaches** the number of parental gametes
- At the limit a **1 : 1 : 1 : 1** ratio: parental = recombinant, the genes are **genetically unlinked** even though they are on the same chromosome
- So recombination frequency can never usefully exceed **50 %**

## Questions
> [!question]- Why can two genes on the same chromosome behave as unlinked?
> If they are far apart, crossovers between them are so frequent that recombinant and parental gametes are equal (1 : 1 : 1 : 1).

> [!question]- What is the maximum recombination frequency between two genes?
> 50 %.
