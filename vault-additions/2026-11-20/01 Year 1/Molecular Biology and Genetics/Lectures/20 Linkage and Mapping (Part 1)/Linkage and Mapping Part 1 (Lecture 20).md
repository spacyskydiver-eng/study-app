> [!key] At a glance
>
> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | Unlinked genes | different chromosomes or far apart: AB : Ab : aB : ab = 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 |
> | Complete linkage | same chromosome, no crossing over: only parental gametes |
> | Incomplete linkage | parental (noncrossover) gametes most common, some crossover gametes |
> | Crossing over | exchange between non-sister chromatids at chiasmata in prophase I |
> | y–w in Drosophila | 98.7 % parental, 1.3 % recombinant |
> | Map distance | 1 cM (centimorgan) = 1 % recombination; y–w = 1.3 cM |
> | Distance rule | further apart → more recombinants; at the limit 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 (unlinked) |
