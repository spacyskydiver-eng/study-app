# Reminder: independent assortment

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage and Mapping Part 1 (Lecture 20)]]

*Source: Lecture 20: Linkage and mapping (Part 1) (PowerPoint), slides 4-7*

- Dihybrid cross: tall yellow × dwarf green → F₁ all tall yellow → F₂ **9 : 3 : 3 : 1**
  - 9 = both dominant traits, 3 = dominant trait 1 + recessive trait 2, 3 = dominant trait 2 + recessive trait 1, 1 = both recessive
- At meiosis I the two chromosome pairs line up **either way round**, so the F₁ makes **Le I, Le i, le I, le i** equally
- This only holds when the genes are on **different chromosomes** (see [[Mendelian Inheritance - Independent Assortment (Lecture 18)]])

## Questions
> [!question]- What F₂ phenotype ratio shows two genes are assorting independently?
> 9 : 3 : 3 : 1.
