# Morgan's Drosophila cross: y and w

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage and Mapping Part 1 (Lecture 20)]]

*Source: Lecture 20: Linkage and mapping (Part 1) (PowerPoint), slides 15-22*

## Drosophila gene nomenclature
- **Dominant** alleles carry a **+**: y⁺; **recessive** have no +: y
- **Wild-type** alleles are usually dominant; **mutant** alleles usually recessive
- **yellow (y)** → yellow body (wild type = grey); **white (w)** → white eyes (wild type = red)
- In cross diagrams, alleles above the line are on one chromosome, below on the other; a **half-arrow** under the line = the **Y chromosome** (male has one X)

## The cross (Thomas Morgan, X-linked genes)
| | Female | Male |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P₁ | y w / y w (yellow, white) | y⁺ w⁺ / Y (grey, red) |
| F₁ | y⁺ w⁺ / y w (grey, red) | y w / Y (yellow, white) |

## F₂
- **Parental 98.7 %**: y⁺ w⁺ and y w combinations
- **Recombinant 1.3 %**: y⁺ w and y w⁺ combinations
- Gametes from the F₁ female: 98.7 % **non-crossover**, 1.3 % **crossover (recombinant)**

## Questions
> [!question]- In Drosophila notation, what does y⁺ mean?
> The dominant (usually wild-type) allele of yellow, giving a grey body.

> [!question]- In Morgan's y w cross, what percentage of F₂ were recombinant?
> 1.3 %.
