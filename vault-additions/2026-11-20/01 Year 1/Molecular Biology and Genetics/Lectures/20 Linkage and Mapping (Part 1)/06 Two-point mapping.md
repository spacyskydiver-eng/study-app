# Two-point mapping

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage and Mapping Part 1 (Lecture 20)]]

*Source: Lecture 20: Linkage and mapping (Part 1) (PowerPoint), slides 25-28*

> [!key] Map unit
> **1 cM (centimorgan) = 1 % recombination** (also called 1 map unit, m.u.)

- **Recombination frequency** = recombinant offspring ÷ total offspring × 100
- y–w: 1.3 % recombinant → **1.3 cM** apart
- **Genetic map**: genes placed along the chromosome by map distance

```
----o--------------y--w--------------------
                   1.3 cM
```

## Questions
> [!question]- Two genes give 8 % recombinant offspring. How far apart are they?
> 8 cM.

> [!question]- Define a centimorgan.
> The distance giving 1 % recombination between two genes.
