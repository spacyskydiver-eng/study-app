# The stages of mitosis

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Chromosomes and Cell Division (Lecture 16)]]

*Source: Lecture 16: Chromosomes and cell division (PowerPoint), slides 6, 9-13*

| Stage | Major events |
| --- | --- |
| **Prophase** | Centrioles migrate to the cell poles; nuclear envelope breaks down; chromosomes condense |
| **Prometaphase** | Chromosomes keep condensing; **spindle fibres** form; chromosomes move towards the centre |
| **Metaphase** | **Centromeres line up** in the centre of the cell (metaphase plate) |
| **Anaphase** | **Centromeres divide**; sister chromatids (now daughter chromosomes) move to opposite poles |
| **Telophase** | Daughter chromosomes arrive at the poles; **[[Cytokinesis|cytokinesis]]** begins (plants build a cell plate) |

- **Interphase** chromosomes are extended and uncoiled (**chromatin**); by **metaphase** they are tightly **condensed**
- Mnemonic: **P**lease **P**ass **M**e **A** **T**owel (prophase, prometaphase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase)

## Questions
> [!question]- In which stage of mitosis do centromeres line up in the middle of the cell?
> Metaphase.

> [!question]- What happens in anaphase?
> Centromeres divide and the sister chromatids are pulled to opposite poles.

> [!question]- When does the nuclear envelope break down?
> Prophase.

> [!question]- What starts in telophase?
> Cytokinesis, the division of the cytoplasm.
