# Summary (Chromosomes and Cell Division)

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Chromosomes and Cell Division (Lecture 16)]]

*Source: Lecture 16: Chromosomes and cell division (PowerPoint), slide 27*

- **Mitosis** replicates somatic cells: daughter cells are **genetically identical** to the parent
- **Meiosis** makes **gametes**, each with **half** the chromosomes of the parent cell
- **Different chromosome assortment patterns** in meiosis give genetic diversity
- **Recombination (crossing over)** in meiosis gives genetic diversity

**Reading:** Klug *et al.*, *Concepts of Genetics* (12th edition), chapter 2
