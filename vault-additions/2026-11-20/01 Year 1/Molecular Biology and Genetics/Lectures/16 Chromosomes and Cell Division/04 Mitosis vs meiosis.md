# Mitosis vs meiosis

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Chromosomes and Cell Division (Lecture 16)]]

*Source: Lecture 16: Chromosomes and cell division (PowerPoint), slides 4, 15, 26*

| | Mitosis | Meiosis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | Somatic cells | Reproductive cells |
| Divisions | 1 | 2 (meiosis I and II) |
| Daughter cells | 2 | 4 |
| Ploidy | Diploid (2n) | Haploid (1n) |
| Genetically | **Identical** to parent | **Different** from parent and each other |
| Homologues pair? | No | Yes, in prophase I (bivalents) |
| Crossing over | No | Yes, prophase I |
| What lines up at metaphase | Single chromosomes | Homologous **pairs** (meiosis I) |
| Purpose | Growth, repair | Gametes for sexual reproduction |

## Questions
> [!question]- Give three differences between mitosis and meiosis.
> Mitosis: 1 division, 2 identical diploid cells, no pairing or crossing over. Meiosis: 2 divisions, 4 genetically different haploid cells, homologues pair and cross over.

> [!question]- At metaphase I of meiosis, what lines up on the metaphase plate?
> Homologous pairs (bivalents), not single chromosomes.
