# Key concepts and reading (Lecture 21)

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage and Mapping Part 2 (Lecture 21)]]

*Source: Lecture 21: Linkage and mapping (Part 2) (PowerPoint), slides 29-30*

- **Linkage** can modify Mendelian phenotype and genotype ratios; segregation still applies but assortment is not independent
- **Incomplete linkage** results from **crossing over** in **prophase I** (= **recombination**)
- Three-point mapping gives **gene order** and **distances**; DCOs reveal the middle gene
- Interference: C = observed DCO ÷ expected DCO; I = 1 − C
