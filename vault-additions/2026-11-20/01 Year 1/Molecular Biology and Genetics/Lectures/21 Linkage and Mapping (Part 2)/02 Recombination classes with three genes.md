# Recombination classes with three genes

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage and Mapping Part 2 (Lecture 21)]]

*Source: Lecture 21: Linkage and mapping (Part 2) (PowerPoint), slides 4-8*

Parent ABC / abc, three genes on one chromosome:

| Class | What happens in prophase I | Gametes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **NCO** (non-crossover) | no crossover | ABC, abc (parental) |
| **SCO^AB** | one crossover between A and B | Abc, aBC |
| **SCO^BC** | one crossover between B and C | ABc, abC |
| **DCO** (double crossover) | one crossover in each interval | **AbC, aBc**: only the **middle gene** swaps |

- NCO are the **most** frequent, DCO the **least** (two independent events have to coincide)
- In a DCO the **middle gene** ends up with the **flanking alleles from the other homologue**

## Questions
> [!question]- Which progeny class is the largest in a three-point cross?
> The non-crossover (NCO, parental) class.

> [!question]- In a double crossover, which allele changes partner?
> The allele of the middle gene; the two flanking genes keep their parental arrangement.
