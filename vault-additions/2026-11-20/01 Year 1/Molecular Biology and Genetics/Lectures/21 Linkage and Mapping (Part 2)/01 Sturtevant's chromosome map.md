# Sturtevant's chromosome map

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage and Mapping Part 2 (Lecture 21)]]

*Source: Lecture 21: Linkage and mapping (Part 2) (PowerPoint), slide 3*

Cross-over frequencies (separate experiments):

| Genes | Recombination |
| --- | --- |
| yellow – white | 0.5 % |
| white – miniature | 34.5 % |
| yellow – miniature | 35.4 % |

- (1) + (2) ≈ (3), so **recombination frequencies are additive**
- yellow and white recombine rarely → **close together**; both far from miniature
- white is closer to miniature (34.5 %) than yellow is (35.4 %) → order **yellow – white – miniature**
- Sturtevant built the first map: **1 map unit (m.u., cM) = 1 % recombination**

```
y-w--------------------m
0.5     34.5 cM
<-------- 35.4 cM ------>
```

## Questions
> [!question]- Why did Sturtevant conclude that recombination frequencies are additive?
> y–w (0.5 %) + w–m (34.5 %) ≈ y–m (35.4 %).

> [!question]- What is the order of yellow, white and miniature?
> yellow – white – miniature.
