> [!key] At a glance
>
> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | Sturtevant | recombination frequencies are additive, so genes can be ordered on a map |
> | Three-point mapping | gives gene order and the distances between three linked genes |
> | NCO | 2 reciprocal classes with the most progeny = parental arrangement |
> | DCO | 2 reciprocal classes with the fewest progeny; the middle gene is the one that swapped |
> | Map distance | % SCO for that interval + % DCO |
> | Coefficient of coincidence | C = observed DCO ÷ expected DCO |
> | Interference | I = 1 − C; positive = fewer DCOs than expected (usual in eukaryotes) |
