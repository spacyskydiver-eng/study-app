# Three-point mapping: the six steps

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Linkage and Mapping Part 2 (Lecture 21)]]

*Source: Lecture 21: Linkage and mapping (Part 2) (PowerPoint), slides 10-24*

> [!key] Three-point mapping finds
> 1. the **order** of three genes on the same chromosome
> 2. the **genetic distances** between them

## The problem
Rabbits: Himalayan **C** > chinchilla **c**; black **B** > brown **b**; white fat **Y** > yellow fat **y**. Test cross **CcBbYy × ccbbyy** (477 progeny):

| Phenotype | Number | Gamete from het parent | % | Class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Himalayan, black, white fat | 151 | C B Y | 31.66 | NCO |
| Chinchilla, black, white fat | 33 | c B Y | 6.91 | SCO^CY |
| Himalayan, brown, white fat | 67 | C b Y | 14.05 | SCO^YB |
| Chinchilla, brown, white fat | 11 | c b Y | 2.30 | DCO |
| Himalayan, brown, yellow fat | 23 | C b y | 4.82 | SCO^CY |
| Chinchilla, brown, yellow fat | 142 | c b y | 29.77 | NCO |
| Himalayan, black, yellow fat | 2 | C B y | 0.42 | DCO |
| Chinchilla, black, yellow fat | 48 | c B y | 10.06 | SCO^YB |

## Steps
1. **Write the gametes** from the heterozygous parent (a test cross shows them directly)
2. **Group into NCO, DCO, SCO**
   - NCO = 2 reciprocal classes with the **most** progeny (151, 142)
   - DCO = 2 reciprocal classes with the **fewest** (2, 11)
   - SCO = the 2 pairs left (33 + 23; 67 + 48)
3. **Arrangement in the het parent** = the NCO classes: **C B Y** and **c b y**
4. **Gene order**: compare NCO (C B Y / c b y) with DCO (C B y / c b Y). Only **Y** has swapped, so **Y is in the middle**: order **C – Y – B**; parent is **C Y B / c y b**
5. **Map distances** = % SCO for that interval + % DCO
   - C–Y: 6.91 + 4.82 + 2.30 + 0.42 = **14.45 cM**
   - Y–B: 14.05 + 10.06 + 2.30 + 0.42 = **26.83 cM**
6. **Draw the map**

```
C-----------Y----------------------B
   14.45 cM        26.83 cM
```
- DCOs are added to **both** intervals because a DCO has a crossover in each

## Questions
> [!question]- How do you identify the DCO classes in a three-point test cross?
> The two reciprocal classes with the fewest progeny.

> [!question]- How do you find the middle gene?
> Compare the DCO with the NCO arrangement; the gene whose allele has switched is the middle one.

> [!question]- How is the map distance for one interval calculated?
> % single crossovers in that interval + % double crossovers.

> [!question]- In the rabbit cross, what are the order and distances?
> C – Y – B; C–Y = 14.45 cM, Y–B = 26.83 cM.
