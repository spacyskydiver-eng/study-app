# Summary (Mendelian Inheritance - Allelic Segregation)

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Mendelian Inheritance - Allelic Segregation (Lecture 17)]]

*Source: Lecture 17: Mendelian inheritance - allelic segregation (PowerPoint), slide 28*

- **Zygosity**: same alleles (Le Le, le le) = **homozygous**; different (Le le) = **heterozygous**
- **Dominance**: Le is dominant to le; le is recessive to Le
- **Law of segregation**: the two alleles separate during gamete formation and end up in different gametes (**allelic segregation**)
- **Phenotype ratio**: monohybrid cross of two true-breeding parents → F₂ **3 : 1** (dominant : recessive)
- **Genotype ratio**: F₂ **1 : 2 : 1** (homozygous dominant : heterozygous : homozygous recessive)

**Reading:** Klug *et al.*, *Concepts of Genetics* (12th edition), chapter 3
