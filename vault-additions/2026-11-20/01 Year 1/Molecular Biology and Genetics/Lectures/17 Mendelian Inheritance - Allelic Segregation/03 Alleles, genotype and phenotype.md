# Alleles, genotype and phenotype

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Mendelian Inheritance - Allelic Segregation (Lecture 17)]]

*Source: Lecture 17: Mendelian inheritance - allelic segregation (PowerPoint), slides 9-10, 15*

- **Gene**: plant height in pea is controlled by the gene **Le**
- **[[Allele|Alleles]]**: versions of a gene. **Le** = tall allele, **le** = dwarf allele. They sit at the **same place** on a **[[Homologous chromosomes|homologous pair]]**
- **Genotype**: the alleles an individual has (Le Le, Le le, le le)
- **Phenotype**: the trait you see (tall, dwarf)

## Zygosity
- **[[Homozygous]]**: two **same** alleles. Le Le = **homozygous dominant**; le le = **homozygous recessive**
- **[[Heterozygous]]**: two **different** alleles, Le le

## Dominance
- **Le** is **[[Dominant allele|dominant]]** to **le**; **le** is **[[Recessive allele|recessive]]** to **Le**
- So **Le Le** and **Le le** are both tall; only **le le** is dwarf

## Questions
> [!question]- What is the difference between genotype and phenotype?
> Genotype is the pair of alleles carried (e.g. Le le); phenotype is the observed trait (e.g. tall).

> [!question]- Which genotype is homozygous recessive: Le Le, Le le or le le?
> le le.

> [!question]- Why are Le Le and Le le plants both tall?
> Le is dominant, so one copy is enough for the tall phenotype.
