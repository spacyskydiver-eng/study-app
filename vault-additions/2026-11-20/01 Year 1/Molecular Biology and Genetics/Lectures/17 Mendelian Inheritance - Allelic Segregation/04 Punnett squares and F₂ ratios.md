# Punnett squares and F₂ ratios

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Mendelian Inheritance - Allelic Segregation (Lecture 17)]]

*Source: Lecture 17: Mendelian inheritance - allelic segregation (PowerPoint), slides 10-14, 20-23*

## The cross written out
- **P₁**: tall **Le Le** × dwarf **le le** → gametes **Le** and **le**
- **F₁**: all **Le le** (tall), making **½ Le** and **½ le** gametes
- **F₂ Punnett square**:

| | **Le** | **le** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Le** | Le Le, tall | Le le, tall |
| **le** | Le le, tall | le le, **dwarf** |

- **Phenotype ratio**: **3 tall : 1 dwarf**
- **Genotype ratio**: **1 Le Le : 2 Le le : 1 le le** (homozygous dominant : heterozygous : homozygous recessive)

> [!example] Worked: using the ratio
> 1000 F₂ plants have **wrinkled** seeds (rr). rr is **1/4** of the F₂, so total ≈ 4000. Heterozygotes Rr are **2/4** = **2000**.
> 1. rr = 1 part → 1000 plants
> 2. Rr = 2 parts → 2 × 1000 = **2000**

## Questions
> [!question]- What gametes does an F₁ Le le plant make, and in what proportion?
> ½ Le and ½ le.

> [!question]- Give the F₂ phenotype and genotype ratios of a monohybrid cross.
> Phenotype 3 : 1 (dominant : recessive); genotype 1 : 2 : 1 (homozygous dominant : heterozygous : homozygous recessive).

> [!question]- 1000 F₂ plants have wrinkled seeds (rr). About how many are heterozygous?
> 2000: rr is 1 part and Rr is 2 parts of the 1 : 2 : 1 ratio.

> [!question]- In an Rr × Rr Punnett square, what is the phenotype of Rr if round (R) is dominant?
> Round.
