> [!key] At a glance
>
> | Term | What to know |
> | --- | --- |
> | Allele | a version of a gene, e.g. Le (tall) and le (dwarf) |
> | Homozygous | two alleles the same: Le Le or le le |
> | Heterozygous | two different alleles: Le le |
> | Dominant / recessive | Le Le and Le le are tall; only le le is dwarf |
> | Monohybrid F₂ | phenotypes 3 : 1; genotypes 1 : 2 : 1 |
> | Law of segregation | the two alleles separate into different gametes (meiosis I) |
> | Test cross | cross to homozygous recessive: all dominant = AA; 1 : 1 = Aa |
