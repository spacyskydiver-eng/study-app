# The monohybrid cross

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Mendelian Inheritance - Allelic Segregation (Lecture 17)]]

*Source: Lecture 17: Mendelian inheritance - allelic segregation (PowerPoint), slides 6-8*

## Tall × dwarf
- **P₁**: tall (~1 m) × dwarf (~0.5 m)
- **F₁**: **all tall**
- **F₂** (F₁ self-fertilised): **787 tall : 277 dwarf** out of 1064 = **2.84 : 1 ≈ 3 : 1**
- Tall is **dominant**; dwarf is **recessive** (it disappears in F₁ and comes back in F₂)

## Every character gave about 3 : 1

| Flower colour | all violet | 705 violet, 224 white | 3.15 : 1 |

> [!tip] Spot the dominant trait
> It's the one **all the F₁** show, and the **bigger** class in F₂. For pods: **full** and **green** are dominant.

## Questions
> [!question]- In tall × dwarf, what are the F₁ and F₂ phenotypes?
> F₁ all tall; F₂ about 3 tall : 1 dwarf (787 : 277).

> [!question]- Which pod traits are dominant: full or constricted, green or yellow?
> Full and green (882 full : 299 constricted; 428 green : 152 yellow).

> [!question]- How can you tell which trait is recessive?
> It disappears in the F₁ and reappears in about a quarter of the F₂.
