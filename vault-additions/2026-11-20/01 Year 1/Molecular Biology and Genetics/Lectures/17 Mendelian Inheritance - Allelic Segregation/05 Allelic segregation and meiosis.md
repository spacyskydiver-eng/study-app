# Allelic segregation and meiosis

> [!path] [[Molecular Biology and Genetics]] › [[Mendelian Inheritance - Allelic Segregation (Lecture 17)]]

*Source: Lecture 17: Mendelian inheritance - allelic segregation (PowerPoint), slides 16-19*

> [!key] Law of segregation (Mendel's first law)
> The **two alleles** for a heritable character **segregate** (separate from each other) during **gamete formation** and end up in **different gametes**.

- This is **[[Allelic segregation|allelic segregation]]**, and **[[Meiosis|meiosis]]** explains it
- In a diploid **Le le** germline cell, Le is on one homologue and le on the other
- After S phase each homologue has two chromatids (Le Le chromatids, le le chromatids)
- **Meiosis I** separates the **homologues**, so **Le and le go to different cells**: this is where segregation happens
- **Meiosis II** separates sister chromatids → **4 haploid gametes: 2 Le, 2 le**

## Questions
> [!question]- State the law of segregation.
> The two alleles of a gene separate during gamete formation and end up in different gametes.

> [!question]- In which division of meiosis do the alleles Le and le separate?
> Meiosis I, when the homologous chromosomes separate.

> [!question]- What fraction of gametes from an Le le plant carry le?
> Half.
